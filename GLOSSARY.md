# Bearers

The language of Bearers, a cooperative tabletop roleplaying game. Each term has one meaning across the Player Rules, the Game Master's Guide, DESIGN.md and the tools. Words under _Avoid_ are never used for that concept.

This file is the source of the Glossary chapter in both handbooks: `tools/sync_glossary.py` copies every term and definition (not the _Avoid_ lines) into them, sorted A–Z, and the PDF build runs it. Write definitions for players; put internal notes in _Avoid_.

## Language

### Characters

**Bearer**:
A humanoid who can etch Runes into themself and take them out again. Every player character is one.
_Avoid_: hero, adventurer, PC (in rules text)

**Party**:
The group of Bearers played together.
_Avoid_: team, group (as a term)

**Game Master (GM)**:
The player who describes the world, runs monsters and adjudicates rules.
_Avoid_: DM, referee

**Ability**:
One of four measures every creature has: Strength (STR), Dexterity (DEX), Intelligence (INT) and Will (WIL), each written as a modifier.
_Avoid_: stat, attribute, ability score, CON, WIS, Charisma, CHA

**Defense**:
The number an attack must reach: 10 + an ability modifier + bonuses. Every creature has a STR, DEX, INT and WIL Defense.
_Avoid_: AC, armor class, save, saving throw

**Totals**:
Your ten running numbers: four ability modifiers, four Defenses, HP maximum and Speed.
_Avoid_: stats, derived stats

### Gear, Runes & Slots

**Gear**:
Every real object a Bearer can equip or store: equipment and items. Any creature can use gear; a Bearer only the cards whose affinities its Calling shares.
_Avoid_: card (outside At the Table), loot (as a synonym)

**Equipment**:
Gear you wear or wield: armor, weapons, shields, Instruments and Jewelry.
_Avoid_: item (for these)

**Item**:
Gear you carry and use: Spellbooks, Recipes, Consumables, Containers and Runestones.
_Avoid_: equipment (for these)

**Object**:
A small non-magical thing that isn't gear: a key, a letter, treasure, rope, a torch. Carried freely, with no limit.
_Avoid_: item, inventory item

**Leather, Mail, Plate**:
The three armors, from lightest to heaviest.
_Avoid_: Light Armor, Medium Armor, Heavy Armor

**Piece**:
One unit of gear, as the Pack, the Party Stash and loot count it. A stack of up to 3 identical Consumables is one piece.
_Avoid_: piece (for WIL magic powers, or Trait-building sources)

**Instrument**:
Equipment held in a Hand that is neither a weapon nor a shield: a lute, a drum, a horn.
_Avoid_: tool

**Focus**:
A keyword on equipment that strengthens your WIL magic: an Instrument, a holy amulet, a druid's staff.
_Avoid_: arcane focus, holy symbol, implement (as the term)

**Container**:
An item that is never equipped: it works from your Pack and takes 1 of its pieces. A Purse holds gold, a *Runestone Bandolier* more Runestones.
_Avoid_: bag, sack (as the term)

**Purse**:
A Container that holds your gold, up to a maximum set by its Rank.
_Avoid_: wallet, coin pouch (as the term)

**Rank**:
The fixed power of a piece of gear or a Rune, from I to V. Characters and Callings have none.
_Avoid_: tier, rarity, level, quality, +1/+2/+3

**Keyword**:
A word with one fixed meaning that gear and Runes list, type first.
_Avoid_: tag, property (as the general term)

**Type**:
The first keyword of gear or a Rune. It names the Slot it fits.
_Avoid_: category, kind

**Slot**:
A space that holds one piece of gear or one Rune of the matching type: Armor, Hand, Jewelry, Item or Rune.
_Avoid_: socket, attunement slot

**Rune**:
A power in the shape of a sign: a Trait, Technique, Domain or WIL deck. Magical creatures nearly always carry a few, anchored in them for life; in others they are the exception (a veteran, a master). A Rune never anchors in a Bearer: only a Bearer can etch one and take it out again. Outside a creature, it sits in a Runestone.
_Avoid_: Aspect, feat, feature, power (as the general term), glyph, sigil (as the term)

**Calling**:
The pair of abilities a Bearer's past has shaped, kept for life: +2 in both, HP, Speed and its own effects and powers. It decides which gear and Runes you can equip. It is neither a Rune nor gear. Two per pair: Duelist and Brawler (STR · DEX), Templar and Patriot (STR · WIL), Spellblade and Tactician (STR · INT), Infiltrator and Ranger (DEX · INT), Performer and Trickster (DEX · WIL), Sage and Hermit (INT · WIL).
_Avoid_: Core, background, archetype, class, race, stat block (for players)

**Affinities**:
The four ability symbols in a card's top corner; the ones it uses are lit. You can equip a card only if your Calling shares at least one of its lit affinities. A weapon attacks with the abilities it names (STR or DEX; INT or WIL for a staff or a wand); basic weapons light all four affinities, except Heavy ones (STR only).
_Avoid_: requirement, prerequisite, Req, symbols (as the term), attunement

**Gear Slot**:
An Armor, Hand, Jewelry or Item Slot.

**Rune Slot**:
A Slot that holds one Rune. You start with 3 and can reach 8.

**Extra Gear Slot**:
A permanent extra Hand, Jewelry or Item Slot, granted only by defeating a Boss: Third Hand, Second Jewel or Great Backpack.
_Avoid_: Slot card

**Slot frame**:
The table component for one Slot: a holder with two corner slits that a card tucks into. A framed card is equipped. A Runestone is a Runestone frame.
_Avoid_: Slot card, sleeve, socket

**Trait**:
A Rune type: a lasting change to your body or mind.
_Avoid_: trait (for any Rune), feature

**Technique**:
A Rune type: a fighting move.

**Domain**:
A Rune type: a bonus on checks for the activities of one domain.
_Avoid_: Knack, skill, proficiency, talent, background

**Effect**:
What a card does on its own, at all times: no action, reaction or use (*Battle Caster*, a bonus to some checks). Every other part of a card's text is a power.
_Avoid_: perk (for a Calling), passive, feature

**Power**:
What a card lets you use: it takes an action or a reaction, or spends a use (*Riposte (½ STR/S.R.)*). WIL magic powers are powers too.
_Avoid_: perk (for a Calling), active, ability (Ability is STR, DEX, INT or WIL), feat

**Uses**:
A limited number of activations of one power, written on it (*2/S.R.*, *1/L.R.*), that come back on a short or long rest.
_Avoid_: charges, per day

### Runestones & Storage

**Runestone**:
A bulky stone that holds one Rune, or nothing (a blank Runestone). An item, but never in a Slot.
_Avoid_: Shard, soul gem, crystal, orb, empty (for a blank Runestone)

**Lingering**:
The state of a dead creature's Runes for 1 minute, once they come loose and before they fade for good.

**Capture**:
Setting a lingering Rune into a blank Runestone.
_Avoid_: harvest, collect, loot (for Runes)

**Gift**:
A copy of a Rune that a living creature other than a Bearer gives willingly, set into a blank Runestone. The giver keeps its own. Only an anchored Rune can be copied, so a Bearer never copies.
_Avoid_: teach, transfer, steal

**Etch**:
Moving a Runestone's Rune into a Rune Slot, during a long rest.
_Avoid_: Absorb, equip (for Runes), learn, attune, engrave

**Release**:
Wiping a Runestone blank. Its Rune is gone for good.
_Avoid_: destroy, discard

**Dormant**:
An etched Rune fallen still: its card lies face down and does nothing, but keeps its Slot. A Rune can go Dormant when a rest heals you (1 in 8 after a short rest, 1 in 4 after a long one).
_Avoid_: broken, faded, inactive, disabled

**Wake**:
At a long rest, releasing a Runestone that holds a Rune of equal or higher Rank, so that a Dormant Rune works again.
_Avoid_: repair, rekindle, recharge

**Pack**:
Your personal bag: up to 8 pieces, gear you carry but haven't equipped and at most 3 Runestones.
_Avoid_: backpack (except *Great Backpack*), bag

**Party Stash**:
The group's mule, cart or wagon: gear and Runestones the party owns but nobody carries.
_Avoid_: bank, vault, storage

### Play

**Story**:
The campaign's big arc, made of quests. Completing one grants a milestone.
_Avoid_: campaign (as a unit), arc, chapter

**Quest**:
A goal the party takes on, a few trials long.
_Avoid_: mission, adventure

**Trial**:
One problem with a clear outcome: a fight, a negotiation, a heist, a hazard, a chase. Each one pays out loot, however it ends.
_Avoid_: challenge (reserved for Challenge Rating), encounter (for non-fights), scene

**Milestone**:
One new Rune Slot for every character, granted when the party completes a story.
_Avoid_: level up, XP

**Play loop**:
The repeating cycle of play: face a trial, gather the loot, rest and rebuild when needed.
_Avoid_: core loop

**Check**:
A roll of d20 + ability modifier + bonuses against a Difficulty Class (DC).
_Avoid_: skill check, test

**Attack roll**:
A roll of d20 + modifiers against a Defense, made by whoever harms or hinders: weapons, spells, WIL magic, traps, poisons.
_Avoid_: saving throw, save

**Advantage on damage**:
Moving an advantage die left after cancelling from an attack to its damage, chosen before rolling: roll the whole damage once more per die and keep the highest total. Disadvantage always stays on the attack.
_Avoid_: damage advantage, extra damage die, brutal

**You question**:
The first of the three questions asked before an attack: your position and conditions (unseen; long range, can't see the target, Prone…). One die at most. The only question asked on checks, saves and area rolls.
_Avoid_: attacker situation, self modifier

**Target question**:
The second of the three questions asked before an attack: the target's conditions (Prone, Restrained, Stunned). One advantage die at most, however many apply.
_Avoid_: target situations (as a count), target modifiers

**Crowd question**:
The third of the three questions asked before an attack: Outnumbered. One advantage die if your side has more creatures engaged around the target, one disadvantage die if its side has more.
_Avoid_: gang-up, flanking, Pack Tactics

**Use**:
The action that uses an object, or gear that takes an action.
_Avoid_: Use Item, Use Object

### Effects

**Subdue**:
The keyword of a spell or power that takes a creature out of the fight. It names its state (Stunned, Charmed, banished…). The target loses its next turn, then the source repeats the attack at each of its turns, with decay, until a repeat misses.
_Avoid_: Hold (as a keyword), Lasting, maintain roll, save-ends, concentration, disable, takedown, crowd control

**Decay**:
The disadvantage dice a Subdue's repeat attack gathers: one for each repeat that hit and each time the source took damage.
_Avoid_: weakening

**Duration**:
A set length of time a spell or power lasts, printed on it.

**Spellbook**:
An item holding a fixed list of spells. Your best equipped book adds its spell points to your one pool. This is INT magic: it uses INT.
_Avoid_: grimoire, spell list

**Spell points**:
The points your spells cost: one pool, what your best Spellbook prints + INT (minimum 1), kept on your tally card. It returns on a long rest.
_Avoid_: spell slots, mana

**WIL deck**:
A Rune of WIL magic: a fixed list of powers, with an Attack bonus and the Resolve it adds to yours. Its type (Divine, Glamour, Nature or Pact) says which powers it holds and changes no other rule.
_Avoid_: prayer book, songbook, WIL book, spell (for its powers)

**Divine**:
A type of WIL deck: the magic of prayer. Its deck is a **Litany**, which holds fate and healing powers: Anthems, Dirges, Cues and Mends.
_Avoid_: Holy Book, Presence, Calling, cleric magic, faith (as the term)

**Glamour**:
A type of WIL deck: the magic of performance and command. Its deck is a **Repertoire**, which holds powers of courage and fear: Anthems, Dirges, Cues and Mends.
_Avoid_: Muse, Presence, Calling, songbook, bardic magic, inspiration

**Nature**:
A type of WIL deck: the magic of the wild. Its deck is an **Instinct**, which holds Forms and Mends.
_Avoid_: Remembrance, Memories, Bestiary, Wilds, Calling, druidic magic, primal magic

**Pact**:
A type of WIL deck: magic granted by a patron. Its deck is a **Pact**, which holds curses and harm: Dirges and Cues.
_Avoid_: warlock magic, Patronage, Grimoire

**Resolve**:
The points your WIL magic costs: one pool, what your best WIL deck prints + WIL (minimum 1), kept on your tally card. It returns on a short rest.
_Avoid_: Presence points, Wild points, will points, mana

**Form**:
One beast body in an Instinct (Nature), printed like a Calling: STR, DEX, HP, Speed and Size. While you are shifted into it, it replaces your body; your mind and Runes stay.
_Avoid_: form (for anything else), shape, wild shape, guise

**Shift**:
To take on a Form from your Instinct; to revert is to return to your own body. Being polymorphed is never shifting.
_Avoid_: transform, polymorph, wild shape

**Spell level**:
A spell's power and its cost in spell points, from 1st to 6th.

**Effective level**:
The GM's estimate of a character's power, from 1 to 12, used to size fights and trial DCs. Not a character stat.
_Avoid_: level (alone), character level

### Keywords

**Armor**:
The type of gear worn on the body; it fits the Armor Slot. Takes 10 minutes to don or doff. Never in the Pack.

**Weapon**:
The type of held gear you attack with; it fits a Hand Slot. It prints its to-hit bonus and damage, adding the higher of the abilities it names (*+2 to hit, 1d8 + STR or DEX Physical*).

**Shield**:
The type of held gear that raises your Defenses; it fits a Hand Slot.

**Jewelry**:
The type of worn gear that fits the Jewelry Slot: rings, amulets, circlets, cloaks, gloves, boots.

**Spell**:
One card of a Spellbook, never on its own. Its level is its cost in spell points.

**Cantrip**:
A spell that costs nothing. Cast at will.

**Consumable**:
An item gone once used. Up to 3 identical Consumables share one Item Slot.

**Recipe**:
An item: a stack that refills at each long rest. Unused items vanish when it refills.

**Action**:
Using it takes one of your two actions.

**Move**:
An action: move up to your speed, split before and after your other action if you like.
_Avoid_: Dash, movement

**Reaction**:
Using it takes your reaction: when the trigger in its text happens, or on your turn if it has none.
_Avoid_: bonus action

**Aquatic**:
A weapon keyword: works underwater, where other weapons and spells don't.

**Brutal**:
A weapon keyword: on a critical hit, roll its damage dice three times instead of twice.

**Crushing**:
A weapon keyword: a hit that beats the targeted Defense by 5 or more knocks the target Prone.

**Flurry**:
A weapon keyword: +2 on each attack after the first you make with it on your turn.

**Heavy**:
A weapon keyword: a big weapon. As loot it lights only the STR affinity, so only a Calling with STR can equip it.

**Light**:
A weapon keyword: allows two-weapon fighting.

**Loading**:
A weapon keyword: one shot per action or reaction, even with extra attacks.

**Noisy N**:
−N on checks to sneak.

**Range N/M**:
A ranged attack. Beyond N: disadvantage. Beyond M: impossible. A single number is a spell's maximum range.

**Reach**:
A weapon keyword: your reach is 10 ft instead of 5 ft.

**Thrown N/M**:
A weapon keyword: it can be thrown as a ranged attack, using the modifier of its melee use. Beyond N ft: disadvantage. Beyond M ft: impossible (*Thrown 20/60*).

**Two-Handed**:
Takes two Hand Slots.

**Versatile**:
A weapon keyword: uses the bigger damage die when held in two hands.

**Targets X**:
The attack is against the target's X Defense (STR, DEX, INT or WIL).

**Area**:
Cone, Cube, Cylinder, Emanation, Line or Sphere N: an area of that shape and size. Roll once against every creature in it. Half damage on a miss.
_Avoid_: AoE, template

**Subtle**:
Nobody notices you casting it.

**Anthem**:
A WIL magic power. Action: a buff for allies who hear you, for a Duration.

**Dirge**:
A WIL magic power. Action: an attack against WIL Defense.

**Cue**:
A WIL magic power. Reaction: changes a roll after it's made, before its result.

**Mend**:
A WIL magic power that restores HP to a creature in range, even one who can't hear you.

**Visual**:
A keyword on a WIL magic power: it needs no voice and acts on creatures who see you, not on those who hear you (a dance, a sign).
_Avoid_: somatic, gesture, Seen (as the keyword)

**Reroll**:
The roll is made again (all its d20s) and the new result counts. A roll can be rerolled only once.

**Resist (type)**:
Take half damage of that type.
_Avoid_: resistance (as a keyword)

**Vulnerable (type)**:
Take double damage of that type.

**Darkvision N**:
Treat Dark as Bright within N ft.

**Blindsight N**:
Perceive everything within N ft without sight.

**Truesight N**:
As Darkvision, and see invisible creatures and through illusions and shapechanges.

**Climb N**:
A climb speed: climbing costs no extra movement.

**Swim N**:
A swim speed: swimming costs no extra movement, and full speed underwater.

**Fly N**:
A fly speed: you move through the air. If you're knocked Prone or Incapacitated while flying, you fall.

**Size**:
Tiny, Small, Medium or Large, printed on a Form or a foe card. Bearers are Medium.

**Wound**:
A lasting injury, drawn at random each time you fall to 0 HP. It stays until a downtime heals it. A Crippling Wound blocks a Gear Slot.
_Avoid_: injury, exhaustion, scar, fatigue

**Downtime**:
3 days of rest in a safe place: does everything a long rest does, and heals all your Wounds.
_Avoid_: long rest (for this), recovery

**Tend**:
At a short or long rest, keeping your Runes instead of healing: you regain no HP, so no Rune can go Dormant. Everything else the rest gives still returns.
_Avoid_: meditate (as a term), attune, maintain

### Monsters

**Boss**:
A strong monster with special powers, including a half turn during the characters' side.
_Avoid_: legendary monster, elite

**Leader**:
A foe keyword: the foe leads its group, and its fall calls for Morale.

**Morale**:
One WIL check (DC 10) a foe group makes, with the best WIL among its remaining members, when its Leader falls and when half the group is down. On a failure, the group flees or surrenders. A Boss never checks.
_Avoid_: rout, nerve, fear check

**Drop**:
Gear or a Rune that a trial yields as loot; to drop is to yield it.
_Avoid_: drop (for falling to 0 HP: "fall")

**Challenge Rating (CR)**:
A monster's danger, used with its XP value to size fights.
_Avoid_: challenge (for anything else)

**Foe card**:
The GM's card for one kind of monster: its body, totals and powers. Cards named in italics on it can drop; plain text never does.
_Avoid_: enemy card, monster card, kit, stat block, Monstrous

**Recharge 5–6**:
A monster power that comes back on a 5 or 6 on a d6 at the end of the monster's turn.

### At the Table

**Card**:
The physical component for a Calling, a piece of gear, a Rune, a spell, a Wound, a foe or an amount of gold.
_Avoid_: card (in rules text outside At the Table)

**Card ID**:
The code in a card's bottom-right corner: type letter, Rank if it has one, and number (W-II-02, L-04).
_Avoid_: card number, serial

**Bearer card**:
The double-width card you fill in ink at character creation: portrait, name, age, folk and look on the front; personality, ambition, fear, bonds, background and notes on the back. It lasts as long as the Bearer.
_Avoid_: character sheet, identity card

**Tally card**:
The card you write on in pencil: your ten totals, HP and temporary HP, death saves, spell points, Resolve and features.
_Avoid_: character sheet (as the term)

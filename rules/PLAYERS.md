## 1. Introduction

**Bearers** is a cooperative storytelling game with an emphasis on character customization and fast-paced mechanics. One player is the **Game Master (GM)**, who describes the world, runs monsters and adjudicates rules. Everyone else plays one character called a **Bearer**; together they form a **Party**. Characters have **no class and no level**: a Bearer is defined by their gear and Aspects, and grows by finding better ones. 

### The Rhythm of Play

Like many tabletop roleplaying games, play is a conversation that repeats three steps:

1. **The GM describes** the scene: where you are, what you see, who is there.
2. **The players say what their characters do.** One player speaks for the group, or each player speaks in turn.
3. **The GM narrates the result.** When the outcome is uncertain, the GM asks for a roll (5.1) first, called a **check**.

In combat the conversation follows turns (6.1); otherwise it flows freely.

### Story, Quests & Trials

The party advances through a **story**, one quest at a time. Play runs at three scales, each inside the next:

- **Story:** the campaign's big arc, made of several quests. It can be a revolt against a tyrant, finding a lost artifact or avenging a betrayed mentor.
- **Quest:** a goal the party takes on: clear the mine, escort the envoy, find the stolen relic. Between its trials, you explore: travel, search and talk your way through the world (5.5, 5.6), and the GM describes what you find. A quest is a few trials long. Completing it may earn a **quest reward**: a Party Stash upgrade, a favor, rare gear or a rare Aspect.
- **Trial:** one problem with a clear outcome: a fight, a negotiation, a heist, a hazard, a chase. Each one pays out, however it ends: equip the new gear and Aspects (3.5) or keep them. Chain trials, and rest and rebuild when the party needs it (7.5).

### Golden Rules

- **The rule of fun.** The game exists for everyone at the table to have a good time. When a rule gets in the way of that, the table can bend it.
- **No one left out.** Every player gets the spotlight. Share scenes, make room for quieter players and give every character something to do.
- **Specific beats general.** Gear, an Aspect or a spell that contradicts a rule wins.
- **Round down** unless told otherwise.
- **Every bonus and penalty stacks**, and so do advantage and disadvantage (5.2). Only conditions don't stack with themselves (Chapter 8).
- **The GM decides** when a rule doesn't cover a situation, and keeps the game moving.

## 2. Characters

### 2.1 The Bearers

Your character is a **Bearer**. A few humanoids carry a gift, a blessing or a curse depending on who you ask: they can **absorb the Aspects** (Traits, Techniques, Knacks, and Divine, Glamour and Nature magic) and the **Core** of other beings. An Aspect has no body of its own. When a creature is defeated, its Aspects linger over the body for a minute, then fade. A Bearer can catch one in a **Shard**: a bulky crystal vessel that holds one Aspect until it is absorbed. Bearers grow by gathering two kinds of resources, each occupying their own **Slots**:

- **Gear:** real objects of two kinds. **Equipment** is what you wear or wield (armor, weapons, shields, Instruments, Jewelry); **items** are what you carry and use (Spellbooks, Recipes, Consumables, Purses, Shards). Gear is equipped in **Armor, Hand, Jewelry and Item Slots**, together called **Gear Slots**.
- **Aspects:** are absorbed in your **Aspect Slots**. The number of a Bearer's absorbed Aspects is limited by the number of Aspects slots it has. This number grows each time the party completes a story (milestones, 4.1). A Shard's Aspect is absorbed while you sleep, during a long rest (3.5). Empty Shards are precious: without one, a lingering Aspect is lost (3.8).
- **The Core** is your body: it sets your abilities, Hit Points and Speed. It is neither an Aspect nor gear, and it takes no Slot. You always have exactly one.

Your look and personality stay the same, whatever Aspects you have equipped. Only a Form (10.7) changes your shape, for a while.

### 2.2 Abilities

Every creature has **four abilities**, each written as a **modifier** (typically −1 to +5), and a **Defense** for each (2.3). Each ability has a symbol; its Defense shows the same symbol inside a shield. Every creature also has **Hit Points** and a **Speed**.

### 2.3 Defenses

- **Defense** = 10 + ability modifier + bonuses (armor, a shield, other gear and Aspects). Every creature has four: **STR, DEX, INT and WIL Defense**. Attacks roll against them (5.1).
  - **Weapon attacks**, melee or ranged, target the defender's **higher** of STR and DEX Defense.
  - **Other effects** name the Defense they target. *A cannon shot targets DEX Defense only: plate doesn't help.*

### 2.4 The Core

The Core is your body. It is neither an Aspect nor gear. You always have exactly one, and it changes only at a long rest.

A Core lists: **STR, DEX, INT, WIL modifiers · Hit Points · Speed · Size** (and sometimes a special effect).

At the table, your Core is a **Core card**, and you write your ten totals on a **tally card** (12.3).

**Player Cores are Small, Medium or Large**; only Rank V gear and Aspects change that. Better Cores (Rank II–IV) drop as loot: higher modifiers, more HP, special effects. The starting Cores are in 13.1.

## 3. Gear, Aspects & Slots

### 3.1 Gear and Aspects

<!-- figure: rank -->

Every piece of gear and every Aspect (a **card** at the table, 12.1) has:

- **A name** (*Warden's Plate*).
- **A Rank** from I to V: its power (*II*).
- **Bonuses** to modifiers, Defenses, HP maximum or Speed (*+4 STR Defense, −10 Speed*).
- **An effect**, if any (*Reaction (1/S.R.), when an attack hits you: reduce its damage by 5*). A power with limited uses shows them on itself: **S.R.** means they come back on a short rest (or a long rest), **L.R.** only on a long rest.
- **Keywords:** the **type** first (it sets the Slot, 3.2), then properties and requirement (*Armor · Noisy 3 · Req STR +2*). See Chapter 11.
- **Requirement** (*Req STR +2*): to equip it, your modifier must be at least that number, counting bonuses from your Core, gear and Aspects, but not its own or any penalty. Fall short later and it stops working until you meet it again.

<!-- figure: card -->

### 3.2 Your Slots

Every character has the same body. Only the number of Aspect Slots grows much.

| Slot | You start with | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Armor** | 1 | One *Armor* |
| **Hand** | 2 | One *Weapon*, *Shield* or *Instrument* each. A Two-Handed weapon takes two Hands. |
| **Jewelry** | 1 | One *Jewelry* (rings, amulets, circlets, cloaks, gloves, boots) |
| **Item** | 2 | One *Spellbook*, *Recipe* or *Purse*, or a stack of up to **3 identical** *Consumables* (potions, scrolls, bombs, oils) |
| **Aspect** | 3 | One *Trait*, *Technique*, *Knack*, *Divine*, *Glamour* or *Nature* |

- A Slot can stay empty.
- Gear or an Aspect fits only the Slot its type keyword names (11.1).
- **Consumables** are gone once used. **Recipes** are the stronger version: a Recipe is a stack that **refills at each long rest**. Unused items from a Recipe vanish when it refills; until then you can hand them to allies.
- **Extra Aspect Slots** come from milestones (4.1). **Extra Hand, Jewelry and Item Slots** come only from Bosses (3.3).
- At the table, each Slot is a **Slot frame**, and the card it holds is tucked into it (12.2).

### 3.3 Extra Gear Slots

Defeating a Boss may grant one character an **extra Gear Slot** for good. The party chooses who.

| Extra Slot | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Third Hand** | +1 Hand Slot (a spectral arm, a familiar, a living tail) |
| **Second Jewel** | +1 Jewelry Slot |
| **Great Backpack** | +1 Item Slot |

- You can have **one of each**, at most. There is never a second Armor Slot.
- An extra Slot **can't be lost, traded or stored**.

### 3.5 Equipping and Swapping

Each Slot has its own swap time. A swap moves one piece of gear between a Slot and your Pack, the Party Stash or the ground. An Aspect moves between its Slot and a Shard (3.8); so does a Core.

| Slot | Swap time | In combat |
|---|---|---|
| **Hand** | Your free object interaction (6.2) | Yes. Dropping a weapon or shield is always free. |
| **Jewelry** | Free, once per turn | Yes |
| **Item: Consumable, Recipe** | Your free object interaction | Yes. Using it costs what it says (drinking a potion: bonus action). |
| **Item: Spellbook** | 1 action | Yes |
| **Armor** | 10 minutes to don or doff | No |
| **Aspect** | Long rest | No |

- **Pack in combat:** taking gear (a Shard included) out of your Pack (or putting it away) costs **1 action and all your movement** for the turn, **plus** the Slot's swap time. The Party Stash can't be reached in combat.
- **Out of combat**, the Pack and the Party Stash cost nothing to reach (the Party Stash only where it is, 3.7). The Slot's swap time still applies.
- **The Core changes only at a long rest.** A captured Core waits in its Shard until then. When you change Core, your HP becomes the new Core's maximum.
- **Expended gear and Aspects are locked:** anything with uses or spell points spent can be swapped out **only at a long rest**.
- Gear and Aspects keep their state in the Pack, the Party Stash and Shards: new loot is fully charged.

### 3.6 Stacking Rules

- You can't equip **two copies** of the same gear or Aspect, Identical Consumables in one stack are fine.
- **Every bonus stacks.** A weapon's to-hit bonus, a Knack's bonus and a Jewelry's bonus all add to the same roll.
- **A power doesn't stack with itself.** Using a power on a creature that is already under it replaces the old one and starts its Duration again. Different powers stack (*Bless* and *Rally*).

### 3.7 Pack, Party Stash, Objects & Gold

- **Pack:** up to **8 pieces of gear**, on your back: weapons, jewels, Spellbooks, Consumables, Purses and **at most 3 Shards** (3.8). **No Armor** (too big). Reachable in combat at a cost (3.5). At the table, a pile of cards beside you (12.2).
- **Party Stash:** up to **5 pieces of gear per character**, shared by the group. It's a mule, a cart or a wagon: it stays **where the party leaves it** (outside the dungeon, at the inn) and can't be reached in combat. The GM may threaten it (thieves, ambush, a river crossing). At the table, a shared pile of cards (12.2).
- **Party Stash upgrades** come only as rewards (quest rewards, Bosses), never from gold. Each replaces the previous one, in pieces per character: *Pack Mule* 8, *Cart* 12, *Wagon* 20.
- **Counting:** each piece of gear counts as 1, Shards (empty or full) and Purses included. A stack of up to 3 identical Consumables counts as 1 (as in an Item Slot).
- **Full Pack or Party Stash:** you can't keep more. Leave the extra piece, give it to an ally, or sell it.
- **Objects:** small non-magical things that aren't gear (treasure, keys, letters, quest objects) are carried freely, with no limit. Ordinary supplies (rope, torches, rations, ammunition, clothes, tools) are **assumed**: you have them when you need them.
- **Money:** gold pieces (gp). Gold buys consumables, services, empty Shards, Purses and, from some merchants, gear and Aspects (sold in their Shard).
- **Purse:** gold is kept in a **Purse**, a piece of gear that holds up to a set amount by Rank: I *Coin Pouch* **100 gp**, II *Merchant's Purse* **500**, III *Banker's Satchel* **2,000**, IV *Guildmaster's Coffer* **10,000**, V *Bottomless Purse* **no limit**. The Purse counts as 1 piece; the gold inside doesn't. Each bag of coins outside a Purse counts as 1 piece. At the table, a Purse is a card, and its gold is **gold cards** tucked under it (12.2).
- **Where it sits:** an Item Slot, your Pack or the Party Stash. The closer to you, the better watched and protected; the GM decides what a thief or a disaster can reach. Gold is lost, stolen and given away like any gear.
- **Paying:** the Purse must be nearby: yours, an ally's, or the Party Stash when the party is with it.

| Common purchases | Cost |
|---|---|
| Potion of Healing (2d4 + 2 HP) | 50 gp |
| Greater Potion of Healing (4d4 + 4 HP) | 150 gp |
| Night at an inn, meals included | 1 gp |
| Rank I gear or Aspect (if a merchant sells it) | 100 gp |
| Rank II gear or Aspect | 500 gp |
| Empty Shard | 250 gp |
| Purse, Rank I / II / III / IV / V | 10 / 50 / 200 / 1,000 / 5,000 gp |

### 3.8 Shards

A **Shard** is an item that holds **one Aspect or Core**, or nothing. It never sits in a Slot: it stays in your Pack or the Party Stash. At the table, a Shard is a **Shard frame**, with the Aspect's or Core's card tucked into it (12.2).

- **Start:** each character has **3 empty Shards**; the Party Stash starts with **2 empty Shards per character**.
- **Bulky:** you carry **at most 3 Shards** in your Pack. Some Jewelry raises this (*Shard Bandolier*). The rest wait in the Party Stash.
- **Lingering:** when a creature is defeated, each of its Aspects and its Core linger over the body for **1 minute**, then fade for good.
- **Capture (1 action):** within 5 ft of the body, pour one lingering Aspect or Core into an empty Shard you carry. The Pack cost (3.5) doesn't apply.
- **Absorb (long rest):** move a Shard's Aspect into an Aspect Slot (a Core replaces your current one). The Shard is now empty.
- **Swap:** the Aspect you take out goes into the Shard the new one came from; no other Shard needed. To take an Aspect out **without** replacing it, you need an empty Shard, or the Aspect is released.
- **Release (free, any time):** empty a Shard. Its Aspect is gone for good.
- **Trade:** a Shard is handed over whole, like any gear: give it, sell it, leave it in the Stash.
- **More Shards:** buy them (250 gp), or find them as loot and quest rewards. Party Stash upgrades (3.7) give more room for them.

> Leather straps padded for crystal. You can carry 2 more Shards in your Pack (5 in all). They still count toward its 8 pieces.
> *Jewelry*

## 4. Progression

You grow stronger mostly through **better gear and Aspects** and a **better Core**, and a little through **more Slots**.

### 4.1 Milestones

A milestone grants **one new Aspect Slot** (up to 8). Every character gains one when the party **completes a story** (Chapter 1).

### 4.2 Other Ways to Grow

- **Harder trials pay more:** tougher enemies, guarded treasure and high-stakes deals carry better gear, Aspects and Cores. Talking, sneaking or tricking your way through pays too: a reward, a favor, a gift.
- **Extra Gear Slots:** granted by Bosses (3.3).

### 4.3 The Endgame

**Rank IV** gear and Aspects start to bend the rules. **Rank V** ones break them: rare Boss treasures that change how the game plays for you or your party. Hunting them is the endgame.

## 5. Playing the Game

### 5.1 Dice, Checks and Attacks

**Dice:** d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100. "3d6" means roll three d6 and add them.

**Reading a d100:** roll two d10s, one marked as tens (often numbered 00–90) and one as ones. Read them together: tens 30 + ones 7 = 37, tens 00 + ones 1 = 1. **Exception:** tens 00 + ones 0 = 100.

The **ability check** is the heart of the game: whenever you try something and the outcome is uncertain, you make one.

1. **Say what you do** and how. The GM decides whether it needs a roll (see Outside combat, below).
2. **The GM picks the ability** that fits the action (2.2) and sets a **Difficulty Class (DC)**: from 5 (very easy) to 30 (nearly impossible); 15 is medium.
3. **Roll d20 + the ability modifier + every bonus** from your gear and Aspects that applies to this action.
4. **Equal to or higher than the DC succeeds.** A failure still moves the story, at a cost.

**Attack rolls:** the **attacker always rolls.** Anything that harms or hinders a creature (a sword, an arrow, a spell, a trap, a breath weapon, a poison) is an **attack roll**: d20 + modifiers against one of the target's **Defenses** (2.3). **Equal or higher hits.**

**Natural 20 / natural 1:** on **attack rolls and maintain rolls** (Chapter 8), a natural 20 always succeeds and a natural 1 always fails.

**Outside combat:**

- **Roll only when** failure is interesting and success is possible. Otherwise just say what happens.
- **Fail forward:** a failed check still moves the story, at a cost: time, noise, damage, a lost resource, a complication. **You can't retry the same task** unless the situation changes.
- **Group checks:** when the whole party attempts something together (sneaking, climbing, searching), everyone rolls. **If at least half succeed, the group succeeds.**
- **Help:** a character who could plausibly assist gives one ally **+3** on the check.
- **Contests:** when two creatures oppose each other, both roll; higher wins; a tie keeps the status quo.

### 5.2 Advantage & Disadvantage

With **advantage**, you roll extra d20s and keep the **highest**. With **disadvantage**, you roll extra d20s and keep the **lowest**. Gear, Aspects, the GM and **situations** grant them. Situations are **location** (long range), **position** (unseen, an enemy within 5 ft of your bow, surprise) and **conditions** (Blinded, Prone, Restrained…).

- **Each source adds one d20.** Each disadvantage cancels one advantage. Roll **1 + what's left** in d20s: keep the **highest** if advantage is left, the **lowest** if disadvantage is left.
- **Situations stack.** *Example: you're unseen and your target is Prone and Restrained: 3 advantage → roll 4d20, keep the highest.*
- **A source counts once**, however many creatures or effects create it. *Exception: Outnumbered (6.5) gives one die per creature one side has over the other.*
- **Natural 20 / natural 1** are read on the kept die.
- **Everything else gives a flat modifier**, such as Help and Dodge (+3 / −3). Flat modifiers stack like other bonuses (3.6) and are independent of dice. Gear or an Aspect that grants an advantage or disadvantage die says so; it counts as a source.

| d20s kept | 6 low | 5 low | 4 low | 3 low | 2 low | 1 | 2 high | 3 high | 4 high | 5 high | 6 high |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Average** | 3.4 | 3.9 | 4.5 | 5.5 | 7.2 | 10.5 | 13.8 | 15.5 | 16.5 | 17.2 | 17.6 |

### 5.3 Passive Awareness

The GM uses **passive Awareness = 10 + INT + bonuses to noticing** to decide what you notice without trying. Each advantage die left adds +5, each disadvantage die −5; flat modifiers add normally. You roll an **INT check** only when **actively searching**.

### 5.4 Hiding

- To hide, you must be **out of sight** of the creatures you're hiding from (behind full cover or in Dark), then make a **DEX check** to sneak.
- **Fail** if the result is below 15 or below the passive Awareness of a creature that could notice you.
- On success, you have the **Invisible** condition against those creatures. It ends when you make a loud noise, attack, cast a spell, or someone finds you (an INT check that beats your result).

### 5.5 Light & Vision

There are two light levels:

| Light | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Bright** | You see normally (daylight, torchlight, dim moonlight, fog that doesn't block sight). |
| **Dark** | You can't see into it. Creatures and objects in Dark are hidden from you, and you are Blinded regarding anything there. |

**Special senses:**

- **Darkvision (range):** you treat Dark as Bright within that range.
- **Blindsight (range):** you perceive everything within range without sight.
- **Truesight (range):** as Darkvision, plus you see invisible creatures and through illusions and shapechanges.

### 5.6 Movement Outside Combat

- **Jumping:** long jump = **10 + 2 × STR** feet; high jump = **3 + STR** feet. Halve both without a 10-ft running start.
- **Climbing and swimming:** each foot costs 1 extra foot of movement unless you have a climb or swim speed. Hard surfaces or rough water call for a STR check.
- **Travel pace:**

| Pace | Per hour | Per day | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast | 4 miles | 30 miles | −5 passive Awareness |
| Normal | 3 miles | 24 miles | — |
| Slow | 2 miles | 18 miles | You can sneak |

## 6. Combat

### 6.1 Order of Combat

1. **Surprise:** the GM decides whether anyone is caught unaware. A side that sneaks up unnoticed (5.4) **surprises** the other and goes first, without rolling.
2. **Side initiative:** if no one is surprised, each side rolls **one d20 + the best DEX among its members**. Re-roll ties. The higher side **goes first**.
3. **Rounds:** the first side takes all its turns, then the second side. Then a new round starts. A round is about 6 seconds. **Bosses** also take a half turn during the characters' side: their movement or one action.
4. **Within a side**, creatures act **in any order** they agree on, one full turn at a time.

**First-mover rule:** in the **first round only**, each creature on the side that goes first gets **either its movement or its action** on its turn, not both. It keeps its bonus action and reaction.

**Ambush:** if the other side is **surprised**, the side that goes first ignores the first-mover rule and takes full turns.

### 6.2 Your Turn

On your turn you can:

- **Move** up to your speed (split before and after your action as you like).
- Take **one action** (table below).
- Take **one bonus action**, only if a rule, gear or an Aspect gives you one.
- Make **one free object interaction** (draw a weapon, open a door, pick up an item).
- Speak briefly.

**Reaction:** one per round, usable on anyone's turn when its trigger occurs. You regain it at the start of your turn.

### 6.3 Actions

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | Make one weapon or unarmed attack (more with Aspects like *Twin Strike*). An attack can be replaced by a Grapple or a Shove. |
| **Cast** | Cast a spell with a casting time of one action, or use a power of your gear or Aspects that takes an action. |
| **Dash** | Gain extra movement equal to your speed this turn. |
| **Disengage** | Your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks this turn. Its cost depends on how many enemies are engaged with you (6.5). |
| **Dodge** | Until your next turn, you have +3 to all your Defenses. Lost if you're Incapacitated or your speed is 0. |
| **Help** | Give an ally +3 on its next check for a task, or on its next attack against a creature within 5 ft of you, before your next turn. |
| **Hide** | Make a DEX check to hide (5.4). |
| **Use** | Use an object, or gear that takes an action. *Drinking or giving a potion is a bonus action.* |
| **Other** | **Ready** (choose a trigger and an action or move; when it happens, use your reaction to do it), **Search** (INT check), **Recall** (INT check), **First Aid** (INT check DC 10 to stabilize a dying creature), **Escape** a grapple, or anything else you describe. |

### 6.4 Movement in Combat

- Combat uses a grid of **5-ft squares**. Each square costs 5 ft, diagonals included.
- **Difficult terrain** (rubble, undergrowth, a creature's space): each foot costs 1 extra foot.
- **Prone:** dropping prone is free. Standing up costs half your speed. Crawling costs 1 extra foot per foot.
- **Other creatures:** you can move through an ally's space (difficult terrain), or an enemy's space only if it's two sizes larger or smaller than you. You can't end your turn in another creature's space.
- **Size and space:**

| Size | Space |
|---|---|
| Tiny | 2½ ft |
| Small / Medium | 5 ft |
| Large | 10 ft |
| Huge | 15 ft |
| Gargantuan | 20 ft or more |

### 6.5 Engagement & Opportunity Attacks

**Engaged:** a creature is engaged with an enemy when it's **within 5 ft** of it, **neither of them is Incapacitated**, and it's **no more than one size larger or smaller** than it. An Incapacitated creature isn't engaged with anyone, and no one is engaged with it. Creatures two or more sizes apart slip past or step over each other (6.4) and don't engage each other.

**Outnumbered:** any attack against a creature (melee, ranged or spell) compares the two sides of the fight around the target:
- **Your side:** every creature on your side engaged with the target, you included if you are.
- **Its side:** the target, if it's engaged with your side, and every one of its allies engaged with your side.

Only engaged creatures count, so an Incapacitated creature counts for neither side. Each creature your side has over its side gives your attack **one advantage die**; each one its side has over yours, **one disadvantage die**. Everyone attacking the same target rolls the same Outnumbered dice, in melee or from afar. This works the same for monsters and characters. *Example: three fighters surround an orc: 3 against 1, so each of them and an archer behind them attack with 2 advantage (3d20, keep the highest). A second orc steps up beside one fighter: 3 against 2, so every attack on the first orc drops to 1 advantage (2d20).*

**Disengage:** the cost depends on how many enemies are engaged with you when you take it.

| Enemies engaged with you | 0–1 | 2 | 3 | 4+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Disengage costs** | Free | Bonus action | Action | Impossible: you're **surrounded** |

**Opportunity attack:** when a creature you can see **leaves your reach** using its movement, you can use your **reaction** to make one melee attack against it. Being pushed, teleported, or Disengaging doesn't provoke.

### 6.6 Making an Attack

1. **Choose a target** within range.
2. **Roll:** d20 + ability modifier + the weapon's **to-hit** bonus + other bonuses.
  - Melee: STR (or DEX for Finesse weapons).
  - Ranged: DEX (thrown weapons use the modifier of their melee use).
  - Spell attack: INT + the book's Spell attack bonus.
3. **Hit** if the total ≥ the target's Defense: the higher of STR and DEX Defense for weapons, the named Defense for other effects. **Natural 20: always hits and is a critical hit. Natural 1: always misses.**
4. **Damage:** weapon die + the same ability modifier you used to attack.

**Critical hit:** roll all of the attack's damage dice twice, then add modifiers once.

**Area effects:** roll **once** and compare the result to each target's Defense. Situations of individual targets and Outnumbered don't add dice to an area roll. On a miss, a damaging area effect deals **half damage** unless it says otherwise.

**Unseen:** attacking a target you can't see gives you disadvantage. Attacking while unseen gives you advantage.

**Unarmed strike:** +2 to hit, 1 + STR Physical. No weapon needed.

**Improvised weapon:** +0 to hit, 1d4 + STR Physical.

### 6.7 Ranged Attacks

- Weapons have a **normal / long range**. Beyond normal range: disadvantage. Beyond long range: impossible.
- Making a ranged attack while an enemy that can see you is **within 5 ft** of you: disadvantage.

### 6.8 Cover

A target behind an obstacle has **cover**: **+2 to all its Defenses.** A target completely behind an obstacle has **full cover** and can't be targeted directly.

### 6.9 Two-Weapon Fighting

When you take the Attack action with a **Light** weapon, you can make **one attack with a different Light weapon** in your other hand as a **bonus action**. Don't add your ability modifier to that attack's damage (unless it's negative). Both weapons must be in your Hand Slots.

### 6.10 Grapple and Shove

Replace one attack with an unarmed **Grapple** or **Shove** against a creature within reach that is **no more than one size larger** than you. Make an unarmed attack (STR + 2) against the target's higher of STR and DEX Defense.

- **Grapple** (you need a free hand): on a hit, the target is **Grappled** by you. You can move it with you, but each foot costs 1 extra foot unless it's two or more sizes smaller than you.
- **Shove:** on a hit, push the target 5 ft away or knock it **Prone**.
- **Escape:** a Grappled creature can use its action to make an unarmed attack (STR or DEX + 2) against the grappler's higher of STR and DEX Defense. On a hit, it's free.

### 6.11 Morale

A foe with the **Leader** keyword leads its group. When a Leader falls, and again when half of its group (rounded up) is down (dead, fled or out of the fight), each remaining member makes a **WIL check (DC 10)**. On a failure, it flees or surrenders, as the GM judges. Each happens once per fight. A **Boss** never checks.

## 7. Damage, Health & Rest

### 7.1 Hit Points

Your **HP maximum** comes from your Core (and some gear and Aspects). Damage lowers your current HP. Healing raises it, up to your maximum.

### 7.2 Damage Types

| Type | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | Blades, arrows, clubs, falls, claws, bites |
| **Fire** | Flames, lava, explosions |
| **Cold** | Frost, freezing water |
| **Lightning** | Electricity, thunder, shockwaves, deafening sound |
| **Poison** | Venom, toxic gas, acid, corrosion |
| **Necrotic** | Life drain, rot, psychic harm, mind blasts |
| **Radiant** | Holy light, searing energy, pure magical force |

**Resistance:** take half damage of that type. **Vulnerability:** double. Apply other modifiers first, then resistance, then vulnerability. Two sources of resistance to the same type count as one.

### 7.3 Temporary HP

- A buffer: damage removes temporary HP first.
- They don't stack: if you gain more, keep the higher amount.
- They aren't healing and can't bring you up from 0 HP.
- They last until you finish a long rest unless an effect says otherwise.

### 7.4 Falling to 0 HP

- **Instant death:** if damage brings you to 0 HP and the **remaining damage ≥ your HP maximum**, you die.
- Otherwise you fall **Unconscious** and start making death saves.

**Death saves:** at the start of each of your turns at 0 HP, roll a d20 (no modifiers).

- **10+:** one success. **9 or less:** one failure.
- **Natural 20:** you regain 1 HP. **Natural 1:** two failures.
- **3 successes:** you're **stable** (unconscious, no more saves; you regain 1 HP after 1d4 hours).
- **3 failures:** you die.
- **Taking damage** at 0 HP is one failure (two on a critical hit). If the damage ≥ your HP maximum, you die.
- **Any healing** brings you back to consciousness and resets your successes and failures. **First Aid** (6.3) stabilizes you.

### 7.5 Resting

|  | Short Rest | Long Rest |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | 1 hour of light activity | 8 hours (at least 6 asleep) |
| **Limit** | 2 per long rest | 1 per 24 hours |
| **HP** | Regain **half your missing HP** (rounded up) | Regain **all** HP |
| **Uses** | "Short rest" uses and Resolve recharge | **All** uses, Resolve and spell points recharge |
| **Other** | Don or doff Armor | Swap Aspects and Core, remove 1 Exhaustion |

A long rest is interrupted by 1 hour of fighting, walking or other strenuous activity. An interrupted rest gives no benefit. You need at least 1 HP to benefit from a long rest.

## 8. Conditions

Conditions don't stack with themselves. Each lasts until its source ends it or its duration expires.

| Condition | Effects |
|---|---|
| **Blinded** | You can't see: you fail any check that relies on sight. Attacks against you have advantage; your attacks have disadvantage. *Deafened (lesser form): you can't hear and fail checks that rely on hearing.* |
| **Charmed** | You can't attack or harm the charmer. The charmer has advantage on WIL checks against you. |
| **Frightened** | Disadvantage on checks and attacks while the source of fear is in sight. You can't willingly move closer to it. |
| **Grappled** | Speed 0. Disadvantage on attacks against anyone except the grappler. Ends if the grappler is Incapacitated or you're moved out of its reach. |
| **Incapacitated** | You can't take actions, bonus actions or reactions. You can't speak. |
| **Invisible** | You can't be seen. Attacks against you have disadvantage; your attacks have advantage. Your side has advantage on initiative if all its members are Invisible. |
| **Poisoned** | Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. |
| **Prone** | You can only crawl or stand up. Your attacks have disadvantage. Attacks against you have one advantage die. |
| **Restrained** | Speed 0. Attacks against you have advantage; your attacks have disadvantage. |
| **Stunned** | Incapacitated, speed 0. Attacks against you have advantage. *Also covers paralysis. Petrified = Stunned + resistance to all damage + turned to stone.* |
| **Unconscious** | Stunned and Prone. You drop what you're holding and are unaware of your surroundings. **Any hit from within 5 ft is a critical hit.** |

### Exhaustion

Exhaustion has **levels 1–6** and stacks.

- Each level: **−2 to all d20 tests** and **−5 ft speed**.
- **Level 6:** you die.
- A **long rest** removes 1 level.

### Lasting Effects and Subdue

A **lasting effect** holds a creature until it breaks free (a web, a curse, a paralysis) rather than for a set time. The attacker keeps rolling to hold it:

- **Maintain roll:** at the start of each of the target's turns, the effect's source rolls **d20 + its ability modifier**, with **no Rank, gear or Aspect bonus**, against the same Defense. On a miss, the effect ends. Monsters use their highest modifier.
- **Hurt source:** the source has **one disadvantage die for each time it took damage** since its last maintain roll.
- The effect also ends if its source is Incapacitated, dies or chooses to end it.

A lasting effect with the **Subdue** keyword takes a creature out of the fight. It names the state it inflicts: **Incapacitated, Stunned, Unconscious, Charmed, banished, polymorphed or Petrified** (*Lasting · Stunned · Subdue*). Subdue adds four rules:

- **First bite:** the target always loses its **next turn** (a Boss: its next turn or half turn, whichever comes first). No maintain roll for that one.
- **Weakening:** each maintain roll after the first adds **one disadvantage die** (second roll: lowest of 2d20; third: lowest of 3d20…).
- **Harming the target:** a **Charmed** target breaks free at once if the source or its allies harm it. For other Subdue effects, **each hit** on the target adds one disadvantage die to the next maintain roll. A **banished** target is gone and can't be hit.
- **Bosses** trigger a maintain roll at the start of their turn **and** of their half turn.

## 9. Equipment

Armor, shields, weapons, Instruments and Jewelry are **equipment**: gear you wear or wield. A weapon prints its **to-hit** bonus and its damage: *+2 to hit, 1d8 + STR Physical*. An **Instrument** (a lute, a drum, a horn) takes a Hand Slot and is usually a **Focus** (10.6).

### 9.1 Armor and Defense

| Armor (gear) | Rank | Bonus | Requirement / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **None** (empty Armor Slot) | — | — | +10 ft speed. |
| **Leather** | I | +2 STR Defense, +1 DEX Defense | +5 ft speed. |
| **Mail** | I | +3 STR Defense | STR +1 |
| **Plate** | II | +4 STR Defense | STR +2. −10 ft speed. Noisy 3. |
| **Shield** | I | +2 STR and DEX Defense | Takes one Hand Slot. |

Armor adds mostly to **STR Defense**. Weapons target your higher of STR and DEX Defense (2.3), so armor protects you when STR Defense is your best.

### 9.2 Weapons

Each row is a kind of Rank I weapon. Its name is flavour: a "Versatile Weapon" can be a longsword, a battleaxe or a spear.

| Weapon (gear) | To hit | Damage | Properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dagger** (dagger, knife) | +4 | 1d4 | Finesse, Light, Thrown (20/60) |
| **Short Blade** (shortsword, sickle) | +3 | 1d4 | Finesse, Light |
| **Light Weapon** (handaxe, club, light hammer) | +2 | 1d6 | Light, Thrown (20/60) |
| **Finesse Blade** (rapier, scimitar) | +2 | 1d6 | Finesse |
| **Versatile Weapon** (longsword, battleaxe, spear, staff) | +2 | 1d8 / 1d10 | Versatile |
| **Heavy Weapon** (greatsword, greataxe, maul) | +2 | 2d6 | Heavy, Two-Handed |
| **Polearm** (glaive, halberd, pike) | +2 | 1d10 | Heavy, Reach, Two-Handed |
| **Whip** | +2 | 1d4 | Finesse, Reach |
| **Javelins** | +2 | 1d6 | Thrown (30/120) |
| **Sling** / **Hand Crossbow** | +2 | 1d6 | Light, Range (30/120) |
| **Bow** (short or long) | +2 | 1d8 | Range (150/600), Two-Handed |
| **Crossbow** (heavy) | +2 | 1d10 | Heavy, Loading, Range (100/400), Two-Handed |

Damage adds the modifier you attack with: STR, DEX for Range weapons, STR or DEX for Finesse ones. Higher-Rank weapons are cards with their own name (*Damascus Sword*, *Katana*), a bigger to-hit bonus and often a special effect. All weapon damage is **Physical** unless the weapon says otherwise.

**Properties** are keywords (Chapter 11): Aquatic, Finesse, Heavy, Light, Loading, Range, Reach, Thrown, Two-Handed, Versatile.

## 10. Magic

### 10.1 Spellbooks

Spells come from **Spellbooks**, a kind of gear. All spellcasting uses **INT**. Each book lists:

- **Spell attack:** the bonus added to INT for its spell attacks.
- **Cantrips:** cast at will.
- **Spells:** a **fixed list**. To change spells, equip a different book.
- **Spell points:** the book's pool, regained on a long rest.
- **Max spell level:** the highest level you can cast (or upcast to) with this book.

**A book holds its spells:** it lists its Rank, spell attack, spell points and max spell level, then its cantrips and spells. It is **one piece of gear** wherever it goes (Item Slot, Pack, Party Stash), and its spells never move to another book. At the table, a book is a **deck**: a cover card and one card per spell (12.4).

A book takes an **Item Slot**, and to cast from it **one of your Hand Slots must be empty** (it holds the book). A Two-Handed weapon fills both Hands. A book's spell points pay only for **that book's** spells. You may equip several books, one per Item Slot.

### 10.2 Spell Points

| Spell level | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cost** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |

- **Cantrips** cost nothing.
- **Upcasting:** pay the cost of a higher level (up to the book's max) to cast a spell at that level.
- **One spell per turn:** you can spend spell points on only one spell per turn (cantrips don't count).
- **High magic:** each book can cast **one spell of 5th or 6th level per long rest**.
- **Extra spell points** from Traits form a small pool of their own: spend them on **any** equipped Spellbook. They return on a long rest.
- Spells range from **1st to 6th level**.

### 10.3 Casting a Spell

- **Casting time:** usually one action (Cast action). Some spells use a bonus action or a reaction.
- **What you need:** the book and an empty Hand (10.1).
- **Obvious or Subtle:** casting a spell is **obvious** (light, sound, a visible gesture) to anyone who can see or hear you, unless the spell is marked **Subtle**. Nobody notices a Subtle spell being cast.
- **Spell attack:** d20 + INT + the book's Spell attack bonus. **Every spell that affects a creature is a spell attack** against the Defense the spell names. Area spells roll once (6.6).
- **Range and targets:** you need a clear path to the target, and the target must be within range. You can target yourself only if the spell says "you" or "a creature" within range.
- **Duration:** instantaneous, timed, or until dispelled.

### 10.4 Lasting Spells

- **A spell on enemies** that holds them (a web, a paralysis, a charm) is a lasting effect: you make its maintain rolls (Chapter 8).
- **Any other spell** (a wall, a blessing on allies, a light) has a **set duration** printed on it. It lasts that long unless it is dispelled or you end it (no action needed).

### 10.5 Areas of Effect

| Shape | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cone** | Widens from you; its width at any point equals its distance from you. |
| **Cube** | Each face is a square of the stated size. |
| **Cylinder** | Circle of the stated radius, stated height. |
| **Emanation** | Extends from a creature in all directions and moves with it. |
| **Line** | Straight line of the stated length and width. |
| **Sphere** | Extends from a point to the stated radius. |

Full cover blocks an area's effect.

### 10.6 Divine, Glamour and Nature

Three kinds of magic draw on **WIL** instead of a book. Each is an **Aspect type**: you capture and absorb it like any Aspect (3.8), it takes an **Aspect Slot**, and it changes only at a long rest. It isn't spellcasting: spell rules (10.1–10.5) don't apply to it.

| Type | Its deck | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Divine** | A **Litany** of prayers | Alters fate (rerolls, fate dice) and heals: Anthems, Dirges, Cues, Mends |
| **Glamour** | A **Repertoire** of songs, poems and dances | Lifts allies and breaks enemies with flat bonuses and penalties: Anthems, Dirges, Cues |
| **Nature** | A **Remembrance** of beasts | Shifts into Forms (10.7) and heals: Forms, Mends |

- **A fixed list:** each one lists its Rank, its Attack bonus, its Resolve, then its powers. To change powers, absorb a different one. At the table, it's a deck (12.4).
- **Resolve:** each has its own pool: the number printed on it **+ your WIL** (minimum 1). It returns on a **short rest** (and a long rest). If your WIL changes, your maximum changes; Resolve already spent stays spent. A power costing **0** is used at will. **Extra Resolve** from Traits and gear forms a small pool of its own: spend it on **any** of your Divine, Glamour or Nature powers; it returns on a short rest.
- **Attack:** d20 + WIL + the printed **Attack** bonus.
- **Be heard:** Divine and Glamour need your voice: you must be able to speak, and targets must hear you (a Glamour dance works on targets who **see** you instead). No Divine or Glamour while Incapacitated, underwater or shifted. Nature needs no voice. None of them needs a Hand, and all are **obvious**.
- **Fate dice:** a d4 at Rank I–II, d6 at III, d8 at IV–V, added to or taken from a roll.
- **Loose powers:** some Aspects carry a single power, with uses instead of Resolve (*Rallying Cry*).
- **Focus:** gear with the **Focus** keyword (an Instrument, a holy amulet, a druid's staff) strengthens these Aspects as it says.

| Power | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthem** | Bonus action | Buffs allies who can hear you, for a **Duration**. No roll. |
| **Dirge** | Action | An attack against **WIL Defense**. Most are **Lasting**: you make the maintain rolls (Chapter 8) with your WIL. |
| **Cue** | Reaction | Changes a roll made by a creature within 60 ft who can hear you, **after the roll** and before the GM says if it succeeds. No roll. |
| **Mend** | As printed | Restores HP. The target needs only to be in range, not to hear you: Mend reaches Unconscious allies. No roll. |
| **Form** | Bonus action | You shift into a beast (10.7). |

### 10.7 Forms
A Remembrance holds **Forms**: beasts your body remembers. A Form is printed like a Core, for the body only: **STR, DEX, HP, Speed and Size**, then its movement, senses and natural weapons. Some print a natural Defense bonus (a hide, a shell). Shifting changes your shape for a while; you keep your mind and personality.
- **Shift (bonus action):** pay the Form's cost in Resolve and take a **fresh tally card** (12.3). Fill it with the Form's STR, DEX, HP, Speed and Size, your **Core's INT and WIL**, and your **Aspects**. Nothing else counts. Shifting from one Form into another costs the new Form's Resolve and a fresh tally card.
- **Your gear merges into the Form:** none of it counts while you're shifted (Armor, Hands, Jewelry, Items, Focus). It comes back in its Slots when you revert, in the same state.
- **Two bodies:** the Form's HP is its own pool. Damage comes off it first; your own HP (and any temporary HP you had) waits on your own tally card.
- **Natural weapons:** attack with the Form's STR or DEX, as printed, + its to-hit bonus, against the higher of STR and DEX Defense, like a weapon. Underwater they count as unarmed.
- **No Hands, no voice:** you can't speak or use Divine, Glamour or Mend powers. Lasting effects you maintain continue.
- **Revert** (free, on your turn) to your own body and tally card. A Form also ends after **1 hour**, or when **its HP reaches 0**: you revert, and damage beyond it carries over to you. Nothing else ends it.
- Forms are **Tiny to Large**. Only Rank V Remembrances hold bigger ones.

## 11. Keywords

A keyword always means the same thing, on every piece of gear and every Aspect. A number or word after it (*Thrown 20/60*, *Resist Fire*) fills in the detail. Conditions (Chapter 8) and damage types (7.2) are also reserved words.

### 11.1 Types

The first keyword. It sets the Slot (3.2).

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Core** | You always have exactly one. Holds the four modifiers, HP maximum, Speed and Size. Changes only at a long rest. |
| **Armor** | Armor Slot. Takes 10 minutes to don or doff. Never in the Pack. |
| **Weapon** | Hand Slot. Attack: d20 + modifier + its printed to-hit bonus, against the higher of STR and DEX Defense. |
| **Shield** | Hand Slot. |
| **Instrument** | Hand Slot. Held gear that is neither a weapon nor a shield: a lute, a drum, a horn. |
| **Jewelry** | Jewelry Slot. Rings, amulets, circlets, cloaks, gloves, boots. |
| **Spellbook** | Item Slot. Holds a fixed list of spells (10.1). Casting needs an empty Hand. |
| **Spell** | Part of a Spellbook, never on its own. Its **level** (1st–6th) is its cost in spell points. |
| **Cantrip** | A spell that costs nothing. Cast at will. |
| **Consumable** | Item Slot, a stack of up to 3 identical Consumables. Gone once used. |
| **Recipe** | Item Slot. A stack that refills at each long rest. Unused items vanish when it refills. |
| **Trait** | Aspect Slot. Something you are: a body, a blood, a blessing. |
| **Technique** | Aspect Slot. Something you trained: a fighting move. |
| **Knack** | Aspect Slot. A bonus on checks for one narrow activity. |
| **Divine** | Aspect Slot. A Litany: prayers that alter fate and heal (10.6). Uses WIL. |
| **Glamour** | Aspect Slot. A Repertoire: songs, poems and dances that lift allies and break enemies (10.6). Uses WIL. |
| **Nature** | Aspect Slot. A Remembrance: Forms and healing (10.6). Uses WIL. |
| **Form** | Part of a Remembrance, never on its own. A beast you shift into (10.7). |
| **Shard** | An item, never in a Slot. Holds one Aspect or Core, or nothing. Pack (3 at most) or Party Stash (3.8). |
| **Purse** | Item Slot, Pack or Party Stash. Holds gold up to a maximum set by its Rank (3.7). |

### 11.2 Use and Timing

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Action** | Using it takes your action. |
| **Bonus action** | Using it takes your bonus action. |
| **Reaction** | Using it takes your reaction, when the trigger in its text happens. |
| **N/S.R.** | Short rest. Written on a power, not a keyword: that power has N uses. All come back on a short or long rest. |
| **N/L.R.** | Long rest. Written on a power, not a keyword: that power has N uses. All come back on a long rest. |
| **Req X +N** | Requirement: you need a total of at least +N in ability X (Core, gear and Aspects, not counting this one; penalties don't count) to equip it. |

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Aquatic** | Works underwater, where other weapons and spells don't. |

| **Heavy** | Needs STR +1 (melee) or DEX +1 (ranged), or −3 on attacks. |
| **Light** | Allows two-weapon fighting (6.9). |
| **Loading** | One shot per action, bonus action or reaction, even with extra attacks. |

| **Range N/M** | A ranged attack. Beyond N: disadvantage. Beyond M: impossible. A single number is a spell's maximum range. |
| **Reach** | Your reach is 10 ft instead of 5 ft. |
| **Thrown N/M** | Can be thrown as a ranged attack, using the modifier of its melee use. Beyond N ft: disadvantage. Beyond M ft: impossible. |
| **Two-Handed** | Takes two Hand Slots. |
| **Versatile (die)** | Uses the bigger damage die when held in two hands. |

### 11.4 Spells and Effects

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Targets X** | The attack is against the target's X Defense (STR, DEX, INT or WIL). |
| **Cone, Cube, Cylinder, Emanation, Line, Sphere N** | An area of that shape and size (10.5). Roll once against every creature in it. Half damage on a miss. |
| **Lasting** | Holds a creature until it breaks free. The source makes maintain rolls (Chapter 8). |
| **Subdue** | A Lasting effect that takes a creature out of the fight; it names the state it inflicts (Stunned, Charmed, banished…). Adds first bite and weakening (Chapter 8). |

| **Subtle** | Nobody notices you casting it. |
| **Focus** | Strengthens your Divine, Glamour or Nature Aspects as printed (10.6). On Jewelry, Instruments or weapons. |
| **Anthem** | Divine or Glamour (10.6). Bonus action: a buff for allies who hear you, for a Duration. |
| **Dirge** | Divine or Glamour (10.6). Action: an attack against WIL Defense. |
| **Cue** | Divine or Glamour (10.6). Reaction: changes a roll after it's made, before its result. |
| **Mend** | Divine or Nature (10.6). Restores HP to a creature in range, even one who can't hear you. |

## 12. At the Table

At the table, everything a Bearer owns is a **card** laid out in front of its player: one card per Core, piece of gear, Aspect, spell and amount of gold, and one **Slot frame** per Slot and Shard. The rules never need the cards; they keep play fast. **No card is ever written on**, except one: your **tally card** (12.3).

### 12.1 Reading a Card

Every card has four parts (see the card in 3.1):

- **Name and Rank** across the top (*Warden's Plate*, Rank II).
- **Bonus strip** down the right side: one row for each of the ten numbers (3.1). The four ability modifiers (STR, DEX, INT, WIL), the four Defenses (the same symbols inside a shield), HP maximum and Speed. Most cards leave it empty. A card that raises an ability modifier also shows the same number in the matching Defense row (+1 STR and +1 STR Defense), so each row adds up on its own.
- **Effect** in the center, if it has one.
- **Keywords** along the bottom, type first.
- **ID** in the bottom-left corner (*W-II-02*): its type, Rank and number, to find it in the box.

### 12.2 Slot Frames and Your Layout

Each of your Slots is a **Slot frame**: a holder a little larger than a card, with two slits at opposite corners. Its top band shows the Slot's symbol, name and swap time (3.5). Its center, hidden under the card, shows what the Slot does when it's empty.

<!-- figure: frame -->

- **Framed means equipped.** Tuck a card's top-right and bottom-left corners under a frame's slits to equip it; untuck it to take it off. Every framed card is in play. Every loose card is in your Pack, the Party Stash or on the ground.
- **Your frames are your Slots.** You start with 9: Armor, 2 Hands, Jewelry, 2 Items and 3 Aspects. The GM hands you a new Aspect frame at each milestone (4.1), and an extra Gear Slot frame when a Boss grants one (3.3). Frames never go into your Pack.
- **Lay out** your frames so every effect and keyword reads: Armor, Hands, Jewelry, Items, Aspects. Your Core and tally card sit beside them, unframed.
- **One card per frame.** A Two-Handed weapon sits in one Hand frame; turn your other Hand frame face down. A stack of Consumables shares one Item frame. A deck (12.4) puts only its cover card in the frame, and its other cards wait beside it.
- **Between sessions,** keep each frame with its card: your build stays as you left it.
- **Pack:** a pile of loose cards beside you. **Party Stash:** a shared pile in the middle of the table, or with the GM when the party leaves it behind.
- **Shards:** a Shard is a **Shard frame**. Tuck the Aspect's or Core's card into it the same way. An empty Shard is the frame alone. Absorbing (3.8) moves the card from its Shard frame into an Aspect frame.
- **Spent uses and spell points:** mark them with tokens on the card.
- **Gold:** gold cards come in **1, 10, 50, 100, 500 and 1,000 gp**. Tuck them under the Purse card that holds them, up to its maximum. A loose gold card is one bag of coins (1 piece). Pay by handing gold cards to the GM, who makes change from the bank.

### 12.3 Keeping Your Totals

Your **tally card** is your **sheet**, and the only card you write on: use a pencil or a dry-erase sleeve, and start a fresh one when it's worn. Its strip lines up with every bonus strip and holds your ten **totals**: the sum of your Core and all your other cards. Beside it, it holds your **current HP**, your **temporary HP** and your **death saves** (7.4).

<!-- figure: tally -->

You only touch the totals at two moments.

**When you swap**, subtract the old card's strip from the total, then add the new card's. An empty Armor Slot adds +10 Speed.

> **Example: Bram swaps armor.** He takes off *Mail* (+3 STR Defense) and puts on *Plate* (+4 STR Defense, −10 Speed). STR Defense: 18 − 3 + 4 = 19. Speed: 30 − 10 = 20.

**Adding up from scratch** (a new Core, or a new tally card): put your tally card down first, then your Core on top of it, one strip to the left. Lay each card that has something in its strip on top of the previous one, one strip to the left, at the same height, so every strip stays visible and the rows line up. Cards with an empty strip stay out. Each row now reads like a written sum, left to right, ending on the tally card, where you write the result. Framed cards stack the same way, frames and all: each card's strip sits on its frame's right edge. An empty Armor frame shows its +10 Speed in the same place.

<!-- figure: totals -->

**When you shift** into a Form (10.7), fill a fresh tally card: the Form's strip, your Core's INT and WIL, then your Aspects (gear doesn't count). Put the Form's HP in its current HP box. When you revert, go back to your own tally card.

**HP:** when your current HP changes, cross out the old number and write the new one.

### 12.4 Decks

A Spellbook is a **deck**: a **cover card** (Rank, spell attack, spell points, max spell level) on top, then **one card per cantrip and spell**, each marked with the book's name. The deck moves as one card, and its spell cards never move to another deck. Mark spent spell points with tokens on the cover. A Litany, a Repertoire and a Remembrance are decks too: a cover card (Rank, Attack, Resolve) and one card per power or Form, marked with spent Resolve tokens. While you're shifted, lay the Form's card on your Core card, with its own tally card.

## 13. Building a Character

### 13.1 Starting Cores

Pick one. All starting Cores are Rank I and Medium size.

| Core | STR | DEX | INT | WIL | HP | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Brute** | +3 | +1 | +0 | +0 | 14 | 30 ft |
| **Nimble** | +0 | +3 | +1 | +0 | 11 | 35 ft |
| **Scholar** | +0 | +1 | +3 | +0 | 9 | 30 ft |
| **Envoy** | +0 | +1 | +0 | +3 | 10 | 30 ft |
| **Balanced** | +2 | +1 | +1 | +0 | 12 | 30 ft |

### 13.2 Creating a Character

1. **Choose a Core** from the starting Cores (13.1).
2. **Choose your gear and Aspects** from the GM's starter list (all Rank I): **1 Armor**, up to **2** for your **Hands** (weapons or a shield), **1 Jewelry** and **3 Aspects**. A caster also picks a **Spellbook** (it takes an Item Slot and needs an empty Hand Slot to cast). A Divine, Glamour or Nature Aspect is one of your 3 Aspects.
3. **Fill your Item Slots**: one *Potion of Healing* (in the free Item Slot).
4. **Take 3 empty Shards** and a *Coin Pouch* (Purse, Rank I) holding **50 gp** (all in your Pack). The party's Stash starts with **2 empty Shards per character** (3.8).
5. **Calculate** your ten totals (four modifiers, four Defenses, HP maximum, Speed) by adding the bonuses of your gear and Aspects to your Core's, and write them on a tally card with your current HP (12.3). Then work out your weapon and spell attack bonuses and passive Awareness (5.3).
6. **Describe** who you are: name, look and a bond.

## 14. Glossary
<!-- glossary:start -->

Each game term has one meaning, in both books.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ability** | One of four measures every creature has: Strength (STR), Dexterity (DEX), Intelligence (INT) and Will (WIL), each written as a modifier. |
| **Absorb** | Moving a Shard's Aspect into an Aspect Slot, or its Core into your body, during a long rest. |
| **Action** | Using it takes your action. |
| **Anthem** | A Divine or Glamour power. Bonus action: a buff for allies who hear you, for a Duration. |
| **Aquatic** | A weapon keyword: works underwater, where other weapons and spells don't. |
| **Area** | Cone, Cube, Cylinder, Emanation, Line or Sphere N: an area of that shape and size. Roll once against every creature in it. Half damage on a miss. |
| **Armor** | The type of gear worn on the body; it fits the Armor Slot. Takes 10 minutes to don or doff. Never in the Pack. |
| **Aspect** | A Trait, Technique, Knack, Divine, Glamour or Nature absorbed from another being. It has no body: outside a Bearer it lives in a Shard. Only Bearers can use it. |
| **Aspect Slot** | A Slot that holds one Aspect. You start with 3 and can reach 8. |
| **Attack roll** | A roll of d20 + modifiers against a Defense, made by whoever harms or hinders: weapons, spells, Divine, Glamour and Nature powers, traps, poisons. |
| **Bearer** | A humanoid who can absorb the Aspects and the Core of other beings. Every player character is one. |
| **Blindsight N** | Perceive everything within N ft without sight. |
| **Bonus action** | Using it takes your bonus action. |
| **Boss** | A strong monster with special abilities, including a half turn during the characters' side. |
| **Cantrip** | A spell that costs nothing. Cast at will. |
| **Capture** | Pouring a lingering Aspect or Core into an empty Shard. |
| **Card** | The physical component for a Core, a piece of gear, an Aspect, a spell or an amount of gold. |
| **Card ID** | The code in a card's bottom-left corner: type letter, Rank and number (W-II-02). |
| **Challenge Rating (CR)** | A monster's danger, used with its XP value to size fights. |
| **Check** | A roll of d20 + ability modifier + bonuses against a Difficulty Class (DC). |
| **Climb N** | A climb speed: climbing costs no extra movement. |
| **Consumable** | An item gone once used. Up to 3 identical Consumables share one Item Slot. |
| **Core** | Your body: four ability modifiers, HP maximum, Speed and Size. You always have exactly one. It is neither an Aspect nor gear. |
| **Cue** | A Divine or Glamour power. Reaction: changes a roll after it's made, before its result. |
| **Darkvision N** | Treat Dark as Bright within N ft. |
| **Defense** | The number an attack must reach: 10 + an ability modifier + bonuses. Every creature has a STR, DEX, INT and WIL Defense. |
| **Dirge** | A Divine or Glamour power. Action: an attack against WIL Defense. |
| **Divine** | An Aspect type: WIL magic of prayer. Its deck, a **Litany**, holds a fixed list of powers that alter fate and heal, and a pool of Resolve. |
| **Drop** | Gear, an Aspect or a Core that a trial yields as loot; to drop is to yield it. |
| **Duration** | A set length of time an effect lasts, printed on it. |
| **Effective level** | The GM's estimate of a character's power, from 1 to 12, used to size fights and trial DCs. Not a character stat. |
| **Equipment** | Gear you wear or wield: armor, weapons, shields, Instruments and Jewelry. |
| **Extra Gear Slot** | A permanent extra Hand, Jewelry or Item Slot, granted only by defeating a Boss: Third Hand, Second Jewel or Great Backpack. |
| **Finesse** | A weapon keyword: use STR or DEX for attack and damage. |
| **Fly N** | A fly speed: you move through the air. If you're knocked Prone or Incapacitated while flying, you fall. |
| **Focus** | A keyword on equipment that strengthens your Divine, Glamour or Nature Aspects: an Instrument, a holy amulet, a druid's staff. |
| **Foe card** | The GM's card for one kind of monster: its Core, totals and powers. Cards named in italics on it can drop; plain text never does. |
| **Form** | One beast body in a Remembrance (Nature), printed like a Core: STR, DEX, HP, Speed and Size. While you are shifted into it, it replaces your body; your mind and Aspects stay. |
| **Game Master (GM)** | The player who describes the world, runs monsters and adjudicates rules. |
| **Gear** | Every real object a Bearer can equip or store: equipment and items. Anyone can use gear. |
| **Gear Slot** | An Armor, Hand, Jewelry or Item Slot. |
| **Glamour** | An Aspect type: WIL magic of performance and command. Its deck, a **Repertoire**, holds a fixed list of powers that lift allies and break enemies, and a pool of Resolve. |
| **Heavy** | A weapon keyword: needs STR +1 (melee) or DEX +1 (ranged), or −3 on attacks. |
| **Instrument** | Equipment held in a Hand that is neither a weapon nor a shield: a lute, a drum, a horn. |
| **Item** | Gear you carry and use: Spellbooks, Recipes, Consumables, Purses and Shards. |
| **Jewelry** | The type of worn gear that fits the Jewelry Slot: rings, amulets, circlets, cloaks, gloves, boots. |
| **Keyword** | A word with one fixed meaning that gear and Aspects list, type first. |
| **Knack** | An Aspect type: a bonus on checks for one narrow activity. |
| **Lasting effect** | An effect on a creature that ends when the creature breaks free, not after a set time. |
| **Leader** | A foe keyword: the foe leads its group, and its fall calls for Morale. |
| **Leather, Mail, Plate** | The three armors, from lightest to heaviest. |
| **Light** | A weapon keyword: allows two-weapon fighting. |
| **Lingering** | The state of a defeated creature's Aspects and Core for 1 minute, before they fade for good. |
| **Loading** | A weapon keyword: one shot per action, bonus action or reaction, even with extra attacks. |
| **Maintain roll** | The source's roll, at the start of each of the target's turns, to keep a lasting effect going. |
| **Mend** | A Divine or Nature power that restores HP to a creature in range, even one who can't hear you. |
| **Milestone** | One new Aspect Slot for every character, granted when the party completes a story. |
| **Morale** | A WIL check (DC 10) that each remaining member of a foe group makes when its Leader falls and when half the group is down. On a failure, it flees or surrenders. A Boss never checks. |
| **Nature** | An Aspect type: WIL magic of the wild. Its deck, a **Remembrance**, holds a fixed list of Forms and healing powers, and a pool of Resolve. |
| **Noisy N** | −N on checks to sneak. |
| **Object** | A small non-magical thing that isn't gear: a key, a letter, treasure, rope, a torch. Carried freely, with no limit. |
| **Pack** | Your personal bag: up to 8 pieces of gear you carry but haven't equipped, at most 3 of them Shards. |
| **Party** | The group of Bearers played together. |
| **Party Stash** | The group's mule, cart or wagon: gear the party owns but nobody carries. |
| **Piece** | One unit of gear, as the Pack, the Party Stash and loot count it. A stack of up to 3 identical Consumables is one piece. |
| **Play loop** | The repeating cycle of play: face a trial, gather the loot, rest and rebuild when needed. |
| **Purse** | An item that holds your gold, up to a maximum set by its Rank. |
| **Quest** | A goal the party takes on, a few trials long. |
| **Range N/M** | A ranged attack. Beyond N: disadvantage. Beyond M: impossible. A single number is a spell's maximum range. |
| **Rank** | The fixed power of a piece of gear, an Aspect or a Core, from I to V. Characters have none. |
| **Reach** | A weapon keyword: your reach is 10 ft instead of 5 ft. |
| **Reaction** | Using it takes your reaction, when the trigger in its text happens. |
| **Recharge 5–6** | A monster power that comes back on a 5 or 6 on a d6 at the end of the monster's turn. |
| **Recipe** | An item: a stack that refills at each long rest. Unused items vanish when it refills. |
| **Release** | Emptying a Shard. Its Aspect or Core is gone for good. |
| **Requirement** | A minimum ability modifier needed to equip gear or an Aspect (*Req STR +2*). |
| **Reroll** | The roll is made again (all its d20s) and the new result counts. A roll can be rerolled only once. |
| **Resist (type)** | Take half damage of that type. |
| **Resolve** | The points a Divine, Glamour or Nature Aspect spends on its powers. Each has its own pool; extra Resolve from Traits can be spent on any of them. It returns on a short rest. |

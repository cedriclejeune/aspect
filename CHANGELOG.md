# Changelog

## v0.1 (draft)
- First standalone core rulebook: `rules/RULES.md`.
- Removed skills: checks are plain ability checks; Aspects grant narrow bonuses or penalties.
- Removed proficiency bonus: replaced by the Rank bonus (+2/+3/+4/+5) of the weapon, spellbook or Aspect used.
- Saves are plain ability saves (STR/DEX/INT/CHA), no save proficiency.
- Side initiative: ambush exception (surprising side takes full turns in round 1).
- Saves have no bonus; monster save DCs scale with CR (10–15).
- All spellcasting uses INT; spellbooks print a Book DC (7–10); spell DC = Book DC + INT.
- Stunned no longer grants automatic critical hits (paralysis folded in).
- Aspect requirements are strict (can't equip without them); Medium Armor needs STR +1 or DEX +1.
- Concentration uses an INT save.
- Saves split between INT and CHA: INT saves against illusions, confusion/madness and concentration; CHA (force of will) saves against charm, fear, mind control, possession, banishment, curses and polymorph. Sample Aspect *Iron Will* added.
- Slot-Granters always grant restricted Slots; granted Slots never hold Slot-Granters.
- Concentration DC capped at 15.
- Loot: 1–3 Aspects per character per encounter; monster Cores are lootable.
- Shatter costs retuned by simulation: 5/8/14/18/20/20/22/22/25/40 (15 Slots ≈ 84 encounters ≈ 5e level 12).
- Merged damage types: resistances/immunities converted per monster, never copied automatically.
- Unconscious: hits within 5 ft are critical hits (5e lethality kept).
- Spells are obvious unless marked Subtle (5e spells without V become Subtle).
- Core changes only at long rest; expended Aspects can be swapped out only at long rest (replaces the fresh-swap rule).
- Side initiative uses the side's average DEX; Finesse weapons deal one die size less.
- Monster passive Awareness: 10 + INT (+5 if alert).
- Advantage/disadvantage stack with no cap (net dice), but only from location, position and conditions. Other sources (Rank I–II Aspects, Help, Dodge) give +3/−3; Rank III–IV Aspects may grant a die. Reckless Attack converts to +3 / +3 against you.
- Engagement (6.5): engaged = within 5 ft, not Incapacitated, within one size. **Outnumbered:** each of the attacker's allies engaged with the target adds one advantage die to any attack (exception to "a source counts once"). Symmetric for monsters; replaces Pack Tactics.
- Disengage cost scales with enemies engaged with you: 0–1 free, 2 bonus action, 3 action, 4+ surrounded (impossible).
- **Slot structure rework:** typed Slots replace untyped ones. Every character has Core, 1 Armor (never more), 2 Hands (weapons/shields), 1 Jewelry (active powers with uses), 2 Items (a Spellbook or a stack of 3 identical consumables) and 3 Attributes (traits, techniques, knacks; up to 8). The Pack is removed.
- Spellbooks take an Item Slot; casting from a book needs an empty Hand Slot (a Two-Handed weapon fills both). **Gestured** spells need two empty Hand Slots (5e: 4th level+ with a Somatic component).
- **Slot cards** (Boss drops, permanent, one of each): Third Hand, Second Jewel, Great Backpack.
- **Shatter removed.** New Attribute Slots come only from milestones (GM grants an Attribute or a better Core). Slot-Granters and the 15-Slot cap removed. Power scaling comes mainly from Rank.
- Bound renamed **Monster Attribute**; monsters don't use the player Slot structure.
- Effective level for encounters is now a GM estimate (Core Rank, Aspect Ranks, open Slots).
- **Swap times per Slot (3.6):** Hand and Consumable = free object interaction; Jewelry = free once per turn; Spellbook = 1 action; Armor = 10 min; Attribute and Core = long rest. Swapping in combat is allowed; taking from or stowing to the Stash in combat costs 1 action + all movement on top. Short rest no longer grants a swap. Aspects may override swap times.
- Jewelry Slot also holds cloaks, gloves and boots.
- **Recipes:** new Item tag. A Recipe is a Consumable stack that refills at each long rest; unused items vanish on refill and can be given to allies meanwhile. Plain Consumables are gone once used. Sample: *Alchemist's Fire Recipe*.
- **Two Stashes (3.8):** Personal Stash (5 Aspects, reachable in combat for 1 action + all movement) and Party Stash (10 Aspects, a mule or wagon left where the party parks it, unreachable in combat). Party Stash upgrades are rewards only: Pack Mule 15, Cart 25, Wagon 40. A stack of 3 identical Consumables counts as 1. Slot swap times still apply out of combat.
- **Loot moment removed:** looted Aspects follow the normal swap times (3.6). GM section 11.2 renamed Loot.
- **Pack** (personal, 5 Aspects, no Armor or Cores) replaces the Personal Stash; the shared one stays **Party Stash**.
- **Boss half turn (11.3):** replaces the Boss's second full turn. Once per round, during the characters' side, between any two characters' turns: its movement or one action. Bosses no longer act twice in a row.
- **Armor trade-offs (9.1):** Medium Armor needs STR +2 or DEX +2 (casters and Envoys fall back on Light). Light Armor gives +10 ft speed; Heavy Armor gives −10 ft speed.
- **Monstrous** replaces Monster Attribute: a tag on a monster's Core or Aspect that never drops. Everything else drops by default (no "lootable" tag). Boss Cores are always Monstrous; looted Cores are player-scaled to the Core table. Player Cores are Small, Medium or Large.
- **Rank V** (Rank bonus +6): game-breaking Aspects, Boss drops only. No Rank V Cores. Rank V spellbook: Book DC 11, 20 points, max 6th, plus one rule-breaking power. Rank IV = slightly game-breaking.
- Boss design guideline (11.5): Core and signature powers Monstrous, drops usually at most one Rank above the party's best.
- **Milestones grant only an Attribute Slot** (the only level-up). Better Cores come from loot only. Characters have no Rank or level; effective level is a GM yardstick for encounters.
- Each Aspect card has one fixed Rank written after its name; stronger versions are separate cards. Two versions of the same Aspect can't be equipped together.
- **Every bonus stacks** (one rule; Rank-bonus and named-modifier limits removed). Balance comes from Rank-sized cards: a card's bonus or penalty = its Rank bonus, a fixed DC on a card = 10 + Rank bonus. Rank I–II Aspects give their Rank bonus instead of advantage (Help and Dodge keep +3 / −3). No cap on stacking: limited Slots are the limit.
- Sample cards renamed with their Rank (*Cat Burglar I*) and resized: *Finesse Blade I* Sap −2, *Ring of Warding I* +2 AC, *Stone Blood I* and *Iron Will I* +2.
- Natural 20 always succeeds and natural 1 always fails on attacks **and saves** (5.1): stacking can't make anyone immune.
- **Defenses replace AC and saving throws.** The attacker always rolls. Every creature has STR, DEX, INT and CHA Defense (10 + modifier + bonuses). Weapon attacks target the defender's higher of STR and DEX Defense; other effects name their Defense. Armor adds to STR Defense (Light +2 and +10 ft, Medium +3, Heavy +4); Shield +2 to STR and DEX Defense. Modifier bonuses improve everything using the ability; Defense bonuses only that Defense.
- Book DC and spell save DC removed: every spell is a spell attack (INT + Rank bonus). Area effects roll once against each target's Defense; a miss deals half damage. Grapple, Shove and Escape are unarmed attacks. Dodge gives +3 to all Defenses; cover +2 to all Defenses. Monster save DC table replaced by an effect bonus by CR (+3 to +8).
- **Lasting effects and Holds (Chapter 8):** the source makes a maintain roll (d20 + modifier only) at the start of each of the target's turns, with one disadvantage die per time it took damage. Holds (fight-ending conditions) add: first bite (target always loses its next turn), weakening (+1 disadvantage die per later roll), harm rules (Charmed breaks at once; hits on other Holds add a disadvantage die; banished can't be hit) and Bosses roll on turn and half turn. Concentration uses maintain rolls (no INT save). **Legendary Resistance removed.**
- Natural 20 / natural 1 apply to attack rolls and maintain rolls.
- **Underwater (5.8):** half speed (unless a swim speed), no spells and no weapons: unarmed attacks only (monsters' natural attacks count as unarmed). No more disadvantage on attacks. New weapon property **Aquatic** (trident, net) and Aquatic Attributes or spells lift the ban. Fire damage is reduced to 0 underwater (was halved).
- **Unarmored:** an empty Armor Slot gives +10 ft speed. Light Armor becomes +2 STR Defense, +1 DEX Defense and +5 ft speed (was +2 STR Defense and +10 ft).
- **No spell components, ever:** costly and consumed materials removed; **Gestured** spells removed (every spell needs only the book and one empty Hand).
- **Concentration removed (10.5):** no one-spell limit and damage never ends spells. Spells holding enemies are lasting effects (maintain rolls); walls, ally blessings and other spells have a set duration.
- Split `RULES.md` into Player Rules (`PLAYERS.md`), Game Master's Guide (`GM.md`, two PDFs) and internal `DESIGN.md`. Design constraints that cards already print (Rank damage scaling, Rank I–II flat bonus vs III+ advantage dice, narrow check bonuses, Finesse die size, power ceiling) and all hidden design notes moved to `DESIGN.md`; redundant "the card says so" lines and fifth-edition comparisons removed from the Player Rules; duplicates merged.
- Player Rules and GM's Guide are fully standalone: removed every mention of fifth edition and every rule phrased as "no X" against it (classes, levels, immunity, ammunition, per-day recharges, advantage cap, concentration, components, Boss resistance to Holds). Monster recharges are now defined in full.
- **Medium Armor (9.1):** requirement is now STR +1 (was STR +2 or DEX +2). DEX characters no longer qualify through DEX.
- **Spellbooks are decks (10.1):** a cover card (Rank, spell points, max level) on top and one card per cantrip and spell, each marked with the book. The deck counts as one Aspect; spell cards never move between books; spent spell points are tokens on the cover.
- **Card layout (3.1):** name and Rank on top, a bonus strip of ten numbers on the right (four modifiers, four Defense bonuses, HP maximum, Speed), effect in the center, keywords at the bottom. Tags are now **type keywords**. Samples (3.4) reformatted; card anatomy figure redrawn.
- **Keywords chapter (11):** every reserved keyword defined in one place (types, use and timing, weapons and armor, spells and effects, senses and movement, monster keywords). Weapon properties moved there from 9.2.
- **Noisy N** keyword: −N on checks to sneak. Heavy Armor II prints Noisy 3.
- **The Core card is the sheet (2.4):** its strip has the Core's own values and a blank **Total** column for the ten sums (pencil or dry-erase sleeve). Character creation writes the totals there.
- **Keeping Your Totals (new 3.8):** spread cards out by Slot to play; a swap subtracts the old strip from the Total and adds the new one (worked example: Bram's armor swap); to add up from scratch, lay the Core first and each card with a non-empty strip on top, one strip left at the same height, so each row reads left to right as a sum ending on the Total. New two-panel figure (in play / adding up).
- **Rank bonus hidden (2.3):** the term is gone from the Player Rules. Cards print their numbers: weapons *Attack +N* (Rank I weapons: +2), spellbook covers *Spell attack: INT + N*, Knacks their bonus. Section 2.3 is now "Rank" (I–V, how powerful a card is); the Rank figure shows the five Ranks without numbers. The bonus table stays in DESIGN.md to size printed numbers.
- **Modifier boosts print twice (3.1):** a card that raises an ability modifier also shows the same number in the matching Defense row, so every strip row sums on its own.
- **Bearers (P 1, GM 6):** lore for the Player Rules. Player characters are Bearers, who absorb the Aspects of defeated creatures. Cores and Attributes are absorbed (they settle during a long rest) and wait as shards when unused; weapons, armor, jewels, spellbooks and consumables are real objects. Look and personality never change, except that a Core's size reshapes the body. Slots are the soul's limit, which grows at milestones. New GM chapter 6, *Bearers in the World*: optional suggestions (stances by region, a mark, the shard trade, rival Bearers, origin, echoes, gifts from willing beings, Monstrous shards). DESIGN.md gains lore constraints.
- **Shards in the World (GM 6.1):** the GM decides how shards fit the world. Suggestions: only Bearers can see and touch them, or everyone can (consequences for theft, trade and society), or a mix. Bearers in Society and Bearers at the Table become 6.2 and 6.3.
- **Rewards beyond combat:** the Core Loop is now *Explore → Adventure → Gather → Rest & Rebuild*; adventures (deals, favors, heists) can earn Aspects too. **Milestones** come at the end of each major story arc (everyone) and when a character completes their goal (that character, who then writes a new goal). GM suggestion (4.1): a loot budget per encounter, not per corpse, whatever the resolution. New social sample Aspects: *Writ of Favor I* (Consumable), *Guild Signet I* (Jewelry), *Renowned I* (Knack).
- **Presence (P 10.6):** a second kind of magic, on CHA: never damage, it buffs and debuffs. You must be heard (no Hand needed, not underwater). Pieces: **Anthem** (bonus action, buff for allies, Duration; Anthems stack like every bonus), **Dirge** (action, Presence attack vs CHA Defense, usually Lasting with CHA maintain rolls), **Cue** (reaction, changes a roll after it's made). Comes as a **Repertoire** deck (Item Slot, Presence points back on a short rest; 0-cost pieces at will) or as loose Attributes with uses. Samples: *Wanderer's Repertoire I*, *Rallying Cry I*. New keywords: Repertoire, Anthem, Dirge, Cue.
- **Healing is CHA:** all healing magic is Presence (**Mend** pieces, which reach Unconscious allies; cost at least 2 points). Presence has **two traditions**: performers give flat bonuses, the faithful alter fate with **rerolls** and **fate dice** (d4 at I–II, d6 III, d8 IV–V). New keywords **Mend** and **Reroll**. Sample: *Acolyte's Litany I* (Guidance, Bless, Bane, Twist Fate, Healing Word, Cure Wounds). Conversion: healing spells → Mend; Bless/Bane/Guidance-style spells → Presence pieces.
- **Presence points = printed + CHA** (minimum 1). A STR character can splash a Repertoire at full power per piece; a CHA character uses it more often. Samples print *3 + CHA*.
- **Classless Aspects / multiclassing:** about half of all Aspects are Core-free (no roll, no modifier). Cards that attack on their own print **two abilities** (*DEX or INT + 3*), use either; weapons and Spellbooks keep one. Requirements only on Medium/Heavy Armor and Heavy weapons. Bridge Aspects link two styles. New samples: *Second Wind I* (Trait), *Battle Hymn I* (Technique), *Spellguard Staff I* (Weapon that counts as an empty Hand for casting). Mend keeps + CHA; Spellbooks unchanged.
- **Cross Traits (P 3.9):** a card that raises an ability modifier always raises two different abilities; no single-ability boost cards. Sizes by Rank: I +1/+1, II +2/+1, III +2/+2, IV +3/+2, V +3/+3. Table of 18 named Traits (Rank I and both Rank II leads for each of the six pairs).
- **Cross Traits reworked (P 3.9):** 12 Traits, each with a lead and a second ability. Rank I: +1 lead plus a perk for the second build (STR +HP max, DEX +Speed, INT +spell points, CHA +Presence points, or a narrow check bonus). Rank II–V: both rise (+1/+1, +2/+1, +2/+2, +3/+2) and the perk grows. Replaces the +1/+1 Rank I table.
- Cross Trait perk points are their own small pool, spendable on any equipped Spellbook (spell points) or Repertoire (Presence points).
- **Trait budget (P 3.9, overarching rule):** for Traits and Knacks, Rank = budget (I 1 … V 5). Bonus (+1 modifier) = 1, perk = 1, strong perk = 2. Never a lone bonus. Strong perks: doubled perk, a sense or movement, a resistance, a small power, levitation, a cantrip. Three families: **Cross** (I perk → V +2/+2 & perk), **Dual** (II +1/+1 → V +3/+2, brought back), **Gifted** (II strong perk → V +2/+1 & strong). Cross Traits lose one point at each Rank. *Second Wind* becomes Rank II (1d10 + 3); *Stone Blood I* loses Resist Poison (over budget).
- **Building Traits moved to the GM's Guide (GM 4.3):** the budget, perks, strong perks and the three families are GM tools for sizing loot. The Player Rules keep only the extra-points rule (10.2, 10.6).
- **Check perks (GM 4.3):** a bonus to one narrow activity costs ¼ of a point; one perk covers up to 4 activities. *Cat Burglar I* (3 activities) fits Rank I.
- **Trait catalog and generator (GM 4.3–4.5):** activities are a piece of their own (¼ each, at most 4 per card, from at least two abilities), with a d6 activity table per ability. Rank I has no bonus or perk; Cross Traits start at Rank II. **Concept Traits (4.4):** 24 named cards, Rank I to V (*Sailor*, *Sellsword*, *Guild Thief*, *Shadow Dancer*, *Legend of the Arena*…), all mixing builds. **Random Traits (4.5):** pair, lead, shape by Rank, activities, strong perk, name.
- **The Play Loop (P 1, GM 1.6):** renamed from the Core Loop (Core is a game term). New section **Stories, Quests & Challenges** before it: a **story** (3–5 quests, ends with a milestone for everyone), a **quest** (3–5 challenges linked by exploration, optional quest reward at the GM's call), and a **challenge**. The loop itself is **Face a Challenge → Gather → Rest & Rebuild**; Explore left the loop (no mechanism of its own). **Personal goals removed:** milestones come only from completing a story, about five per campaign.
- **Lexicon (P 12):** the Terminology table moved to the end of the Player Rules as its own chapter.
- **Shards are vessels (P 1, 3.7, 3.8):** an Aspect never takes a physical form. When a creature is defeated its Aspects and Core linger 1 minute; a Bearer captures one into an **empty Shard** they carry (1 action, within 5 ft, no Pack cost). Absorbing (long rest) empties the Shard; a swap reuses it; removing without replacing needs an empty Shard. Release is free; Shards are traded whole. Each character starts with **3 empty Shards**, the Party Stash with **2 per character**. Shards count as cards; you carry **at most 3** (new Jewelry *Shard Bandolier I*: +2). Empty Shards cost **250 gp** and drop as loot or quest rewards. Cores can now be carried (in a Shard).
- **Pack 8 cards** (was 5). **Party Stash 5 cards per character** (was 10); upgrades Pack Mule 8, Cart 12, Wagon 20 per character. Keeping Your Totals becomes P 3.9.
- **Gear and Aspects, not cards:** the rules now say *gear* and *Aspects* (or *loot* for rewards); "card" appears only in the new **At the Table** chapter (P 12: reading a card, laying out, keeping totals on the Core card, spellbook decks). Chapter 3 is now **Gear, Aspects & Slots**. **Slot cards** become **extra Gear Slots** (P 3.3), a permanent Boss reward; Armor, Hand, Jewelry and Item Slots are together the **Gear Slots**. Pack and Party Stash count **pieces of gear and Shards**. Lexicon is now chapter 13.
- **Stat symbols (P 2.1):** fist (STR), feather (DEX), eye (INT), radiant star (CHA); each Defense is its symbol in a shield; heart (HP), boot (Speed). They sit in the 2.1 tables (build inlines `rules/symbols/*.svg`); 2.1 now also defines HP and Speed.
- **Tally card (P 12.3):** the Core card loses its Total column; a card-sized **tally card** is now the sheet and the **only card ever written on** (disposable, pencil or dry-erase sleeve). Its strip lines up with the bonus strips and holds the ten totals; beside it, current HP, temporary HP and death saves. To add up from scratch, lay the tally card first, then the Core one strip to the left, then the other cards. New figure; Core and Keeping Your Totals figures redrawn.
- **Purses and gold cards (P 3.7, 11.1, 12.2):** gold is gear. A **Purse** holds up to 100 / 500 / 2,000 / 10,000 gp / no limit (Rank I–V) and costs 10 / 50 / 200 / 1,000 / 5,000 gp. It counts as 1 piece; its gold doesn't; each loose bag of coins is 1 piece. It sits in an Item Slot, the Pack or the Party Stash (the GM judges how exposed), and paying needs it nearby. At the table, gold cards of 1, 10, 50, 100, 500 and 1,000 gp tuck under the Purse card. Each character starts with a **Purse I holding 50 gp** in the Pack. New keyword **Purse**; Lexicon gains Tally card and Purse.
- **Design constraints (DESIGN 4):** never write on cards except the tally card; in-universe text never says "card", only table-setup text does.
- **One word, one meaning (`GLOSSARY.md`):** a canonical vocabulary at the repo root, each term with the words to avoid. The **Glossary** is now the last chapter of both handbooks (P 14, GM 7), generated from it by `tools/sync_glossary.py` (run by the PDF build), so the two stay identical. It replaces the Lexicon; Building a Character is now P 13.
- **Renames:** *challenge* → **trial** (the unit of play; Challenge Rating keeps "challenge") · Presence *pieces* → **verses** ("piece" now only counts gear) · the *Hold* keyword → **Subdue** (cards name the state: *Lasting · Stunned · Subdue*) · *Light / Medium / Heavy Armor* → **Leather / Mail / Plate** · *Use Item* → **Use** · *Dropping to 0 HP* → **Falling to 0 HP** ("drop" is the loot term) · GM 4.3–4.5 cover **Traits and Knacks** ("Trait" is only the type) · "Shard" is always capitalised.
- **The Core is not an Aspect (P 2.1, 2.4):** it's your body, neither an Aspect nor gear.
- **Gear = equipment + items (P 2.1, 9):** **equipment** is worn or wielded (armor, weapons, shields, Jewelry); **items** are carried and used (Spellbooks, Repertoires, Recipes, Consumables, Purses, Shards). Shards are now gear, so the Pack and Party Stash count only pieces of gear.
- **Objects (P 3.7):** small non-magical things that aren't gear are carried freely; the 10 + STR inventory limit is gone.
- **Armor figure (P 9.1)** redrawn with Defense bonuses (it still showed AC).
- **Every card is unique (P 3.1, 3.4, 3.6, 3.7; GM 4.3–4.5; DESIGN 4):** names never carry a Rank numeral; a stronger card gets a new name. Samples show the Rank beside the name (*Plate* · Rank II). Purses by Rank: *Coin Pouch*, *Merchant's Purse*, *Banker's Satchel*, *Guildmaster's Coffer*, *Bottomless Purse*; you start with a *Coin Pouch*. *Wiry III* example renamed *Whipcord*. The "two copies at different Ranks" stacking case is gone.
- **Card anatomy (P 3.1):** name and Rank are separate bullets, every bullet uses *Warden's Plate* as its example; the Rank ladder is now vertical, beside the list.
- **Cards named where concepts live (P 2.4, 3.1, 3.7, 3.8, 10.1, 10.6; DESIGN 4):** each concept a card represents gets one short "At the table…" mention where it's defined (Core card and tally card, gear and Aspect cards, Pack and Stash piles, Purse and gold cards, Shard card, Spellbook and Repertoire decks). The 3.2 Slot table drops its Maximum column; 3.1 drops the roll table.
- **Uses sit on the power (P 3.1, 3.4, 11.2; GM 3, 4.3–4.4; DESIGN 4):** limited uses leave the keywords and become a tag on the power they limit: **Reaction (1/S.R.)**, or **2/S.R.** opening a power with no action type. The 3.1 Uses bullet folds into Effect. Monster *N/day* becomes *N/L.R.*; *Recharge 5–6* stays. Spell points, Presence points and Recipe flasks are whole-card pools and stay put.
- **Charisma → Will (WIL)** (P 2.1 and everywhere): the fourth ability is now **Will**. It covers social checks and force of will, and it powers Presence and healing; **WIL Defense** resists charm, fear, mind control, possession, banishment, curses and polymorph. Symbols are now `wil.svg` / `wil-def.svg` (same radiant star). The DESIGN conversion table maps Charisma and CHA saves → WIL.
- **Will magic becomes Aspects (P 10.6):** Presence is gone. WIL powers its own three Aspect types, each a deck absorbed like any Aspect (Aspect Slot, long-rest swap, Shards): **Divine** (a *Litany*: alters fate and heals), **Glamour** (a *Repertoire* of songs, poems and dances: flat bonuses and penalties) and **Nature** (a *Remembrance*: Forms and healing). Repertoires leave the Item Slot. Points are **Resolve** (printed + WIL, short rest; extra Resolve from Traits spends on any of them). Powers keep their kinds (Anthem, Dirge, Cue, Mend); *verse* is dropped. Only Divine and Nature heal. Divine and Glamour need your voice (a Glamour dance needs only to be seen); Nature doesn't.
- **Forms (P 10.7):** shift into a beast from a Remembrance (bonus action, 1–3 Resolve). A Form gives temporary HP, sets your Size and Speed (Speed bonuses from gear and Aspects still apply), adds its bonuses, natural weapons (WIL or a fitting physical ability) and senses. No Hands, Items, voice, Divine, Glamour or Mends while shifted; Armor and Jewelry keep working. It ends when its temporary HP is gone, after 1 hour, on a free revert, at Unconscious or when polymorphed. Tiny to Large below Rank V. New keywords **Fly N** and **Tiny**. Sample *Wildheart's Remembrance*.
- **Instruments and Focus (P 9, 11):** new Hand type **Instrument**; new keyword **Focus** (Jewelry, Instruments, weapons) for gear that strengthens Divine, Glamour and Nature. Sample *Traveler's Lute*.
- **Parity (DESIGN 5, GM 4.1):** every build's engine costs one Slot; Aspect Slots are the shared growth track, and loot shares Aspects evenly between styles. A WIL starter takes its Divine, Glamour or Nature Aspect as one of its 3 Aspects. The GM's starter list includes a Litany, a Repertoire, a Remembrance and a Focus.
- **Glossary:** Divine, Glamour, Nature, Resolve, Form, Shift, Instrument and Focus added; Presence and Verse removed; Aspects "have no body of their own" (was "no physical form").
- **Slot frames (P 3.2, 3.8, 12.2, 12.3; GM 4.2; DESIGN 4):** every Slot is a physical **Slot frame** (67 × 98 mm) that a card tucks into by two corner slits (top right, bottom left). **Framed = equipped**; loose cards are in the Pack, the Party Stash or on the ground. A character starts with 9 frames; the GM hands out a new Aspect frame at each milestone and an extra Gear Slot frame from a Boss. The top band shows the Slot's symbol, name and swap time; the center shows what an empty Slot does (empty Armor: +10 Speed, in its strip row). The card's strip sits on the frame's right edge, so framed cards still stack to add up. Shards are **Shard frames**. New figure in 12.2; glossary gains **Slot frame**. DESIGN records the future **special Slots** (a loot frame that replaces a plain one; its bonus belongs to the Slot).
- **Base set (`rules/Bearers-Base-Set.pdf`):** a printable 4-player test kit built by `tools/build_cards.py` from `cards/base_set.py`: the 5 starting Cores and 2 Rank II Cores, a Rank I starter list for every style (GM 4.2 table), a Rank I–II loot pool, the Conjurer's Book, Wanderer's Repertoire, Acolyte's Litany and Wildheart's Remembrance as decks, gold cards, tally cards, and 63 Slot and Shard frames. New cards: *Holy Amulet*, *Boots of the Long Stride*, *Clear Mind*, *Cleave*, *Steady Aim*, *Shield Bash*, *Boarspear*, *Ring of Sparks*; the Conjurer's Book spells get full texts. Weapon cards are specific weapons (*Dagger*, *Shortsword*, *Handaxe*, *Longsword*, *Battleaxe*, *Greatsword*, *Glaive*, *Longbow*), and every card has a simple vector drawing (`tools/card_art.py`).
- **Forms are body-only Cores (P 10.7, 12.3):** a Form prints STR, DEX, HP, Speed and Size like a Core (plus movement, senses, natural weapons and sometimes a natural Defense bonus). Shifting fills a **fresh tally card**: the Form's body, your Core's INT and WIL, and your Aspects; **gear merges and is off** (was: Armor and Jewelry kept working). The Form's HP is its **own pool** (was: temporary HP); damage beyond it carries over to you. Natural weapons use the Form's STR or DEX (was: WIL or a physical ability). - **Aspects become Runes (P 2.1, 3.8, 10.6, 10.7, 11.1; GM 2.1, 4, 6; DESIGN 1b):** playtesters found the fiction creepy (Bearers stole the memories and skills of the beings they beat), so the fiction changes and no number does. A **Rune** is a power in the shape of a sign; a Bearer **etches** it and takes it out again; it waits in a **Runestone** (a *blank* one holds nothing). Renamed: Aspect → Rune (Rune Slot), Shard → Runestone, Absorb → Etch, Knack → **Domain**, Remembrance → **Instinct**; Capture, Lingering and Release keep their names. French: *Rune*, *Pierre runique* (*Pierre vierge*), *Graver*, *Domaine*, *Instinct*.
  - **Naming rule (DESIGN 1b):** a Rune names a power, never a person, a race, a memory or a lived experience. 24 cards renamed with the same numbers and IDs: *Human Grit* → *Grit*, *Elven Blood* → *Dusk Eyes*, *Dwarven Blood* → *Bedrock*, *Halfling Luck* → *Luck*, *Orcish Blood* → *Raid*, *Hobgoblin Discipline* → *Discipline*, *Pack Hunter* → *Wolfpack*, *Wiry* → *Sinew*, *Relentless* → *Defiance*, *Slippery* → *Sidestep*, *Sellsword* → *Bravado*, *Acrobat* → *Tumbling*, *Hedge Witch* → *Herblore*, *Cat Burglar* → *Burglary*, *Street Urchin* → *Backstreets*, *Renowned* → *Renown*, *Sailor* → *Waves*, *Hunter* → *Hunt*, *Physician* → *Remedies*, *Barkeep* → *Tavern*, *Smith* → *Forge*, *Mountaineer* → *Peaks*, *Wildheart's Remembrance* → *Wildheart's Instinct*, *Shard Bandolier* → *Runestone Bandolier*. The concept Traits and Domains of GM 4.3–4.5 follow the same rule (*Diplomat* → *Diplomacy*, *Spy* → *Espionage*…). All lore lines rewritten.
  - **Species has no card:** a Bearer's species is description only, and any humanoid can carry the six Rank I Runes that began as species cards (GM 2.1).
  - **Lingering and gifts (P 3.8; GM 4.1, 6.3):** Runes come loose when a creature **dies**. New **Gift**: a living, willing creature that isn't a Bearer gives a **copy** of one of its Runes and keeps its own (a master, a patron, a foe who surrenders); a Bearer never copies.
  - **Anchoring (P 2.1, 3.8; GM 6.3):** in most creatures a Rune is anchored; in a Bearer it never anchors. So a Bearer can take a Rune out, can't copy it, and keeps nothing of it once it is out. Drawing a Rune's sign does nothing: the sign is only its trace.
  - **GM Guide 6:** 6.1 is now *Runes in the World*: three possible origins, and a new row on whether etched Runes show on the body. *Hunted* Bearers get no stated motive. Echoes belong to Rank IV and V Runes only; lower Runes are mute tools.
  - **Cards:** a Rune's type line reads *Rune · Trait*; the Runestone frame replaces the Shard frame. Drawings were renamed with their cards, not redrawn. The simulator plays the renamed cards (159 tests pass); its labels and wording still say Aspect until the next pass.
- **The Rank is carved (P 3.1):** on every card, the Rank in the circle becomes a branch rune in a stone, one branch per Rank (three on the right, then on the left). The Rank figure in the Player Rules shows the same marks.
- **Rune names and "Rune of" (P 3.1; GM 4; DESIGN 1b):** every Rune card prints *Rune of* / *Rune du, de la, de l', des* in small above its name, and its type alone below. Renamed, same numbers and IDs: *Burglary* → *Larceny*, *Renown* → *Minstrel*, *Waves* → *Navigation*, *Remedies* → *Healing*, *Herblore* → *Herbalist*, *Raid* → *Campaign* (French: *Larcin*, *Ménestrel*, *Navigation*, *Guérison*, *Herboriste*, *Campagne*). Trade names were tried and dropped: only *Minstrel* and *Herbalist* keep one.
- **One WIL magic (P 10.6, 10.7; DESIGN 3):** Divine, Glamour and Nature now follow the same rules, and the type is only a flavor. Every **WIL deck** prints the same cover (Attack, Resolve, one flavor line), and spends the same Resolve; only the powers a deck holds differ by type, as before. One voice rule: every power needs your voice except shifting into a Form. So *Wildheart's Instinct* prints *Attack: WIL + 2*, *Mossbalm* needs your voice, *Acolyte's Litany* no longer prints its fate die on the cover, and the Glamour dance exception becomes the **Visual** keyword, printed on the power that has it (none in the base set yet). New glossary term: *WIL deck* (*paquet de VOL*). INT magic stays the long-rest magic and WIL magic the short-rest one (DESIGN 3).
- **Sidestep leaves the set (DESIGN 4):** no card may duplicate a Calling perk, and *Sidestep* doubled the Duelist's *Footwork*. The Goblin keeps the move as its own ability, *Nimble (2/S.R.)*, and carries only its Sling, Dagger and Leather. The tally card example lists *Grit* instead.
- **Battle Hymn turns defensive (DESIGN 4):** on a weapon hit, up to 3 allies within 30 ft who can hear you have +2 to STR and DEX Defense until the start of your next turn (was +2 on their next attack, which outdid the Ranger's *Quarry*). The Hobgoblin carries it, so its line now shields instead of sharpening.
- **New perks for the two DEX · WIL Callings (DESIGN 4):** their old perks did what several cards do. Performer: *Presence* (checks to perform, persuade and deceive add both DEX and WIL) replaces *Crowd Work*. Trickster: *Sure Thing* (on checks to sneak, pick locks, pick pockets and disarm traps, a d20 under 10 counts as 10; a hit on a creature that can't see you deals +½ DEX or ½ WIL d6 damage, the higher) replaces *Feint*. *Disarm traps* joins the DEX checks.
- **Hiding ends after the first attack (P 5.4):** Invisible from hiding ends right after your first attack; if an action gives several attacks (*Twin Strike*), only the first is made while hidden.
- **Starter expansion (P 9.1, 10.6; GM 4.2; DESIGN 4):** 81 starter cards instead of 43. New gear: *Hide*, *Studded Leather*, *Whip*, *Hand Crossbow*, *Crossbow*, *Druid's Staff*, *Druid's Totem*, *Arcane Crystal*, *Healer's Kit*. New Runes: fighting styles (*Archery*, *Bulwark*, *Dueling*, *Great Weapons*, *Quick Throw*), signatures (*Rage*, *Cunning*, *Unarmored Guard*, *Martial Arts*, *Expertise*, *Arcane Recovery*), Domains (*Library*, *Court*, *Frontier*, *Deceit*, *Asceticism*). Four Spellbooks: *Evoker's Book*, *Abjurer's Book*, *Illusionist's Book*, *Enchanter's Book*. A fourth WIL deck type, the **Pact** (*Occultist's Pact*: *Eldritch Blast*, *Enfeeble*, *Hellish Rebuke*, *Hex*). The *Acolyte's Litany*, *Wildheart's Instinct*, *Shortbow*, *Greataxe*, *Greatclub*, *Wand* and *Holy Amulet* move from loot to the starter list. The Rune Slot frame now reads "A Trait, Technique, Domain or WIL deck."
- **Symbols become Affinities (P 3.1; glossary):** the four lit ability symbols in a card's top corner are now called **Affinities** (FR *Affinités*). You equip a card if your Calling shares at least one of its lit affinities. *Symbol* now means only the drawing.
- **Effects and powers (P 3.1; glossary):** card text is either an **effect** (FR *effet*: works on its own, at all times) or a **power** (FR *abilité*: an action, a reaction or a use). A Calling no longer has a "perk" (FR *atout*): it has its own effects and powers. FR keeps *pouvoir* for WIL magic powers. The Trait budget (GM 4.3) keeps *perk / atout*.
- **Player Rules trimmed and reordered (EN and FR):** about a quarter shorter: the sample cards are gone, examples and repeated rules cut. Chapters now follow the order of definitions (DESIGN, Order of definitions): 3 Playing the Game (with a new 3.3 Time: Turns and Rests), 4 Gear, Runes & Slots (Pack and Runestones before Equipping), 5 Equipment, 6 Combat (the three questions move to 6.6, ranged attacks too), 7 Damage, 8 Conditions, 9 Magic (durations in 9.3), 10 Progression (with Extra Gear Slots). Chapters 11 to 14 keep their numbers. References updated in the GM's Guide, DESIGN, the cheat sheets, the figures and the sim. GM-facing rules move to the GM's Guide: roll-only-when and fail forward (GM 1.1), travel pace (GM 1.2), Morale (new GM 2.4; P 6.10 is gone).
- **Dormant Runes: Tend or heal (P 2.1, 7.5, 12.2; GM 4.1; DESIGN 5; glossary):** at any rest you can **Tend** (FR *Méditer*): no HP back, everything else returns. If a rest heals you, roll 1d8 (short rest) or 1d4 (long rest); on a 1, one etched Rune of your choice goes **Dormant** (FR *dormante*): face down, does nothing, keeps its Slot. **Wake** (FR *Réveiller*) it at a long rest by releasing a Runestone holding a Rune of equal or higher Rank. Downtime and healing outside a rest never roll. Cheat sheets updated.
- **Hide runs, Mail guards (P 5.1; DESIGN 4):** *Hide* gains +5 ft Speed (FR +2 m), so it no longer reads as a weaker *Mail*: same +4 HP, Mail +3 STR Defense, Hide +2 and faster.
- **Best book, best deck (P 9.2, 9.5; DESIGN 4):** spell points = the best equipped Spellbook + INT, Resolve = the best WIL deck + WIL. A second book or deck adds only its spells or powers, never points. Sim: two-book and two-deck builds lose points; the Hawk test now expects 5 Resolve.
- **Taking a creature out costs at least 2 (DESIGN 4):** *Sleep* and *Charm Person* move to 2nd level; the *Enchanter's Book* gains *Heroism* (1st: can't be Frightened, 2 temporary HP at the start of each turn), played in the sim (`tempTurn`, `noFear` mods).
- **FR: le Pouvoir (DESIGN 4):** *magie de Volonté* becomes **le Pouvoir**, *paquet de Volonté* **Rune de Pouvoirs**; *pouvoir* always means one of its cards (a Rune is *une force en forme de signe*, monsters have *abilités*). New glossary entry *Pouvoir*.
- **Fifteen classic monsters for new parties (GM 2.2):** new foe cards converted from the SRD 5.2.1 (fifth-edition CR 1/8 to 3): Stirge, Giant Centipede, Boar, Flying Sword, Gnoll, Shadow, Goblin Boss (Leader), Dire Wolf, Ghoul, Bugbear, Animated Armor, Harpy, Brown Bear, Gelatinous Cube and the Owlbear Boss.
